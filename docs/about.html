About Us

Last updated: 17 November 2026

All Is Well Rashifal is the astrology website of Pandit Raju Pathak. It offers daily horoscopes, upcoming planetary transits (gochar), today's panchang, fasts and festivals, name-letter love horoscope and a free kundli (birth chart).

Pandit Raju Pathak

Pandit Raju Pathak is based in Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh, India. He runs the YouTube channel "All Is Well Rashifal" with about 5.7 million subscribers, and has about 1.5 million followers each on Facebook ("All Is Well Rashifal") and Instagram ("Raju Lakshmi Pathak").

How we calculate

Panchang, planetary positions and transit dates are computed with the Swiss Ephemeris using the Lahiri ayanamsa; Rahu/Ketu use the mean node, as in traditional panchangs.

Services

Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly horoscopes • transit alerts • free birth chart • detailed kundli report (₹2100, booked on WhatsApp) • information on gemstones, rudraksha, malas, yantras and havan samagri.
