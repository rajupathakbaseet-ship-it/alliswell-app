Marriage Muhurat 2027: Complete Month-wise List of Auspicious Wedding Dates with Time, Nakshatra and Tithi

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: 2027 has 96 marriage muhurat days. They begin on 15 January; May has the most (18). There are no weddings during Kharmas (about 15 March–14 April) or Chaturmas (14 July–10 November). हिंदी में पढ़ें

For a wedding, the date matters as much as the match. A muhurat is chosen when the Sun, Moon, Jupiter and Venus are favourable, the nakshatra and tithi are auspicious, and there is no Kharmas, Chaturmas or Venus/Jupiter combustion. Here is the complete list of marriage muhurats for 2027.

All dates and times are for New Delhi (IST), matched with the Drik Panchang. Timings in your city may differ by a few minutes.

Marriage Muhurats 2027: Month-wise Count

There are 96 marriage muhurat days in 2027: January 9, February 13, March 7, April 9, May 18, June 16, July 6, November 11, December 7. May has the most. The first muhurat of the year is on 15 January, right after Kharmas ends.

When There Are No Weddings in 2027

  • Kharmas (Meen Malmas): about 15 March to 14 April 2027, when the Sun is in Pisces.
  • Chaturmas: from Devshayani Ekadashi, 14 July, to Dev Uthani Ekadashi, 10 November 2027.
  • Months not listed below (August, September and October) have no auspicious marriage date.

How to Choose the Right Date for Your Wedding

  • A general muhurat is not enough – check it against the bride's and groom's Moon signs (chandra bal and tara bal).
  • Avoid dates when either family faces a death-related impurity (sutak).
  • For a night wedding, make sure the pheras fall inside the muhurat window.
  • Book the venue early for May and November–December, when demand is highest.

🙏 Pandit Raju Pathak's Advice

Pandit Raju Pathak says – "A general muhurat is a starting point, not the final word. Always match the date with the bride's and groom's horoscopes, and keep the pheras inside the muhurat time – that matters more than the grandeur of the function."
