December 2026 Hindu Festivals and Vrat List: Utpanna Ekadashi, Gita Jayanti, Dattatreya Jayanti, Kharmas and Rahu-Ketu Transit

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: December 2026 has two Ekadashis – Utpanna on 4 December and Mokshada (Gita Jayanti) on 20 December. Vivah Panchami is on 14 December, Kharmas starts on 16 December and Dattatreya Jayanti is on 23 December. Rahu-Ketu change signs on 5 December and Saturn turns direct on 11 December. हिंदी में पढ़ें

December 2026 is a busy and sacred month in the Hindu calendar. It opens in the holy month of Margashirsha (Agahan) – the month Lord Krishna calls his own in the Gita – and ends in Paush. It brings two Ekadashis, Gita Jayanti, Vivah Panchami, Dattatreya Jayanti, the start of Kharmas and two big planetary moves: Rahu-Ketu change signs and Saturn turns direct.

All dates and times are for New Delhi (IST), matched with the Drik Panchang. Timings in your city may differ by a few minutes.

Ekadashis in December 2026

Utpanna Ekadashi – Friday, 4 December: the Ekadashi of Margashirsha Krishna Paksha. It is believed that Goddess Ekadashi was born from Lord Vishnu on this day, so people who want to start keeping Ekadashi fasts often begin with this one.

Mokshada Ekadashi and Gita Jayanti – Sunday, 20 December: on this day Lord Krishna gave the teaching of the Bhagavad Gita to Arjuna at Kurukshetra. Reading the Gita, donating and fasting on this day is considered especially meritorious.

Margashirsha Amavasya – Tuesday, 8 December

Amavasya is the day of the ancestors. A holy dip, tarpan for the ancestors and charity are recommended. In the evening, light a mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree.

Vivah Panchami – Monday, 14 December

Lord Ram and Goddess Sita were married on Margashirsha Shukla Panchami. Ayodhya and Janakpur (Nepal) celebrate it in grand style. Reading the Ram-Sita wedding chapter of the Ramcharitmanas at home is a lovely way to mark the day.

Dattatreya Jayanti and Margashirsha Purnima – Wednesday, 23 December

Lord Dattatreya, believed to embody Brahma, Vishnu and Mahesh together, was born on Margashirsha Purnima. Annapurna Jayanti also falls on this day – donating food grains is especially good.

Kharmas from 16 December: a Month Without Auspicious Ceremonies

When the Sun enters Sagittarius (Dhanu) on 16 December 2026, Kharmas begins. It lasts until the Sun enters Capricorn on Makar Sankranti, 14 January 2027. During Kharmas, weddings, engagements, griha pravesh, mundan and new ventures are avoided, while worship, charity and pilgrimages are encouraged.

Big Planetary Moves in December 2026

5 December: Rahu moves from Aquarius to Capricorn and Ketu from Leo to Cancer – a change that stays for about a year and a half. 11 December: Saturn turns direct in Pisces, so delayed work starts moving again.

🙏 Pandit Raju Pathak's Advice

Pandit Raju Pathak says – "December is a month of devotion. Read at least one chapter of the Gita on Gita Jayanti, and during Kharmas put your energy into worship and charity instead of new beginnings. Plan weddings and housewarmings before 16 December or after 14 January."

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Kharmas in December 2026?

Kharmas begins on 16 December 2026 when the Sun enters Sagittarius and ends on 14 January 2027 (Makar Sankranti).

When is Gita Jayanti 2026?

Gita Jayanti and Mokshada Ekadashi fall on Sunday, 20 December 2026.

How many Ekadashis are there in December 2026?

Two – Utpanna Ekadashi on 4 December and Mokshada Ekadashi on 20 December.

When is Margashirsha Amavasya 2026?

On Tuesday, 8 December 2026.
