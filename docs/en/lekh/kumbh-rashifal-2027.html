Aquarius Horoscope 2027 (Kumbh Rashifal 2027): End of Sade Sati, Marriage Yogas, Career, Money and Month-by-Month Predictions

All Is Well Rashifal • Pandit Raju Pathak, Jhansi (India) • 17 November 2026 • हिंदी में पढ़ें

For people with the Moon in Aquarius (Kumbh rashi), 2027 is a year of relief after a long wait. Saturn's Sade Sati, running since January 2020, will almost end on 3 June 2027, when Saturn moves into Aries, your 3rd house. The obstacles, financial pressure and mental burden of the past years will clearly ease this year. From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter enters your 7th house and directly aspects your sign – an auspicious time for marriage, partnership and respect. Rahu stays in the 12th house all year affecting expenses and sleep, and Ketu in the 6th house works against opponents. Pandit ji says – 2027 is a year of new beginnings; leave the old tiredness behind and move forward.

Aquarius 2027 at a Glance

• Best period: 3 June to 20 October 2027 (Saturn in the 3rd house) and 26 June to 26 November (Jupiter in the 7th house)
• Period to be careful: 5 July to 23 August (Mars in the 8th house), from 26 November (Jupiter in the 8th house) and from 27 December (Mars in the 12th house)
• Expenses: Rahu in the 12th house all year – foreign matters, travel and expenses increase
• Victory over opponents: Ketu in the 6th house – relief from cases, illness and debt
• Sade Sati: relief on 3 June 2027; the last part runs again from 20 October to 22 February 2028

Now let us see which house of Aquarius each of these planets occupies and what results it gives.

When Will Aquarius Sade Sati End – The Biggest News of 2027

This is the biggest question for Aquarius natives. At present Saturn is in Pisces, the 2nd house from Aquarius – this is the last phase of Sade Sati. According to the panchang, Saturn moves into Aries on 3 June 2027 and you get relief from Sade Sati. But Saturn turns retrograde on 9 August and returns to Pisces on 20 October 2027, so the last part of Sade Sati runs again from 20 October to 22 February 2028. On 23 February 2028 Saturn moves into Aries for good and Aquarius Sade Sati ends completely.

In the last phase, the tests relate to money, family and speech. During this time keep money accounts clean, do not speak harshly in the family and watch your diet.

Career and Job 2027

From 3 June to 20 October Saturn stays in the 3rd house – a very good position for Saturn. Courage increases, you progress through your own effort, and work stuck for years moves forward. From 25 January to 25 June Jupiter stays in the 6th house, and Ketu is in the 6th house too – work will demand more effort, but opponents weaken and you succeed in competitive exams. From 10 March to 25 April Mars in the 6th house also gives victory over opponents.

From 8 October to 17 November Mars stays in the 10th house – the fastest period for promotion, a new post and authority at work. Rahu in the 12th house gives job opportunities abroad, in a big company or in a distant city.

Business 2027

From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter in the 7th house brings new partnerships, big clients and trustworthy associates – a good time to grow your business. Rahu in the 12th house gives gains from import-export, online trade and foreign customers, but also increases needless expenses. From 18 November to 26 December Mars in the house of gains gives good profit.

Money and Finances 2027

At the start of the year, with Saturn in the house of wealth, money comes slowly and saving feels hard. From 3 June this pressure eases. Rahu in the 12th house increases expenses all year – money will go on travel, treatment, foreign matters or the home, so set aside a savings portion first every month. From 18 November to 26 December Mars in the house of gains gives a good time for earnings. From 26 November Jupiter in the 8th house can give both sudden expenses and sudden gains.

Love Life 2027

At the start of the year, with Saturn in the house of wealth, you may feel some distance in love or restrictions from family. The good news is that from 26 June to 26 November Jupiter in the 7th house helps love relationships reach marriage. Those who are single may find a good partner now. Rahu in the 12th house can pull you towards secret relationships or a partner living far away – keep honesty in the relationship.

Marriage 2027

For unmarried Aquarius natives, 26 June to 26 November 2027 is the best time for marriage – Jupiter sits in the 7th house and aspects your sign. For those whose marriage was stuck because of Sade Sati, this is a big opportunity. For married people, the spouse may get progress during this time and sweetness in the relationship increases. At the start of the year (until 9 March) Mars is in the 7th house – avoid arguments then. Fix the wedding date through kundli matching and an auspicious muhurat.

Family and Home 2027

At the start of the year Saturn in the house of wealth increases family responsibilities. From 3 June you get support from brothers and sisters, and gains from short trips. From 18 November to 26 December Mars in the house of gains brings help from elder siblings. Rahu in the 12th house creates chances of living away from home or going abroad.

Health 2027

Ketu in the 6th house gives relief from old illness and makes treatment effective. Rahu in the 12th house may cause lack of sleep and trouble with feet and eyes – avoid staying up late and spending too much time on screens. From 5 July to 23 August Mars stays in the 8th house – drive very carefully then and avoid injury. At the end of the year Jupiter in the 8th house asks for attention to the stomach and sugar. Keep getting regular check-ups.

Studies and Exams 2027

2027 is very good for competitive exams – Jupiter and Ketu in the 6th house reward hard work. After 3 June Saturn in the 3rd house gives the strength to work steadily. Rahu in the 12th house can fulfil the dream of studying abroad. From 24 August to 7 October Mars in the house of fortune is good for higher education.

Aquarius 2027 Month by Month

January: last phase of Sade Sati – be patient. Jupiter in the 6th house from 25 January – victory over opponents.
February: more effort at work, but sure results.
March: Mars in the 6th house from 10 March – victory in cases, competition and over illness.
April: Jupiter direct from 13 April. Mars in the 7th house from 26 April – do not argue with partners.
May: keep money accounts clean and make new plans.
June: relief from Sade Sati from 3 June. Jupiter in the 7th house from 26 June – marriage and partnership yogas.
July: Mars in the 8th house from 5 July – care with vehicles and health.
August: Saturn retrograde from 9 August. Mars in the house of fortune from 24 August – travel and higher education.
September: a very good month for marriage, engagement and new partnerships.
October: Mars in the 10th house from 8 October – chances of promotion. Saturn back in the house of wealth from 20 October.
November: Mars in the house of gains from 18 November – income rises. Jupiter in the 8th house from 26 November.
December: Mars and Rahu in the 12th house from 27 December – watch expenses and sleep.

Aquarius 2027: Lucky Numbers, Colours and Days

• Lucky numbers: 8 and 7
• Lucky colours: blue, sky blue, grey and purple
• Lucky days: Saturday and Friday
• Ruling planet: Saturn (Shani)

Easy Remedies for Aquarius 2027

1. For Sade Sati, light a mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree every Saturday and chant "Om Sham Shanaishcharaya Namah".
2. For Rahu, donate black sesame, blankets or footwear on Saturday.
3. Recite the Hanuman Chalisa every Tuesday and Saturday.
4. Donate yellow things on Thursday – marriage yogas get stronger.
5. Help labourers, the elderly and people in need – this pleases Saturn the most.

Dos and Don'ts in 2027

✅ Do:
• Decide on a new beginning, new job or business after June.
• Finalise marriage and partnerships between June and November.
• Set aside savings first every month, then spend.
• Put full effort into competitive exams and old legal cases.

❌ Don't:
• Keep the habit of staying up late and spending needlessly.
• Drive fast or take risky jobs in July–August.
• Be careless in the last part of Sade Sati after October.
• Spoil family relations with harsh words.

Pandit ji's Important Note

This horoscope is a general prediction based on the Moon sign and the planetary transits of 2027. Every person's kundli, dasha and antardasha are different, so show your kundli before a big decision. For Aquarius, 2027 is a year of coming out of Sade Sati and making a new start – move forward with the experience Saturn has taught you.
