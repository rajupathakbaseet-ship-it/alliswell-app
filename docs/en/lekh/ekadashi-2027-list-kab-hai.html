Ekadashi 2027 Date List: All 25 Ekadashi Dates, Tithi Timings, Fasting Rules and Parana

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 25 November 2026WfXT
In short: There are 25 Ekadashis in 2027. The first, Saphala Ekadashi, is on Sunday 3 January and the last, again Saphala Ekadashi, is on Thursday 23 December. Nirjala Ekadashi is on 14 June, Devshayani Ekadashi on 14 July and Dev Uthani Ekadashi on 10 November 2027. All dates are matched with the New Delhi panchang (IST).

"When is Ekadashi?" – millions of people ask this every month. In the Hindu calendar two Ekadashis come every month, one in the Krishna (waning) paksha and one in the Shukla (waxing) paksha. In North India it is also called Gyaras. Ekadashi is dedicated to Lord Vishnu and its fast is counted among the most meritorious vrats.

Special Ekadashis of 2027

Nirjala Ekadashi 2027 – 14 June, Monday

The Ekadashi of Jyeshtha Shukla is Nirjala Ekadashi. The tradition is to fast without even water, so it is the hardest Ekadashi. It is believed that this one fast gives the merit of all Ekadashis of the year. It is also called Bhimseni Ekadashi. People with health problems should take fruit or water on their doctor's advice.

Devshayani Ekadashi 2027 – 14 July, Wednesday

On Ashadha Shukla Ekadashi Lord Vishnu goes into yoga-nidra and Chaturmas begins. During Chaturmas, marriage, griha pravesh and mundan are not done. See Marriage Muhurat 2027 →

Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2027 – 10 November, Wednesday

On Kartik Shukla Ekadashi Lord Vishnu wakes up and Chaturmas ends. Wedding muhurats begin again after this. Tulsi Vivah is done on this day or the next in many places.

Mokshada Ekadashi and Gita Jayanti 2027 – 9 December, Thursday

On Margashirsha Shukla Ekadashi Shri Krishna gave the teaching of the Gita to Arjuna. Reading the Gita and giving charity are especially meritorious on this day.

Smarta and Vaishnava Ekadashi

Sometimes the Ekadashi tithi spans two days. Then householders (Smarta) fast on the first day and the Vaishnava tradition (ISKCON etc.) on the next. In 2027 this happens three times: Pausha Putrada Ekadashi (Smarta 18 January, Vaishnava 19 January), Kamika Ekadashi (Smarta 29 July, Vaishnava 30 July) and Rama Ekadashi (Smarta 25 October, Vaishnava 26 October). The list above is for householders.

Ekadashi Fasting Rules

• Eat simple sattvic food from the night of Dashami and do not eat rice on Ekadashi.
• Bathe in the morning, worship Lord Vishnu, offer Tulsi leaves and chant "Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya".
• Fast on fruit or without food, as your capacity allows. The elderly, the sick and pregnant women should fast according to their health.
• Avoid lies, anger and gossip; bhajan-kirtan at night is considered auspicious.
• Break the fast (parana) the next day within Dvadashi tithi, after giving charity.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When is the next Ekadashi?

The next Ekadashi is Dev Uthani Ekadashi on Friday, 20 November 2026, then Utpanna Ekadashi on 4 December and Mokshada Ekadashi on 20 December. The first Ekadashi of 2027 is Saphala Ekadashi on 3 January.

How many Ekadashis are there in 2027?

There are 25 Ekadashis in 2027. Saphala Ekadashi comes in both January and December.

When is Nirjala Ekadashi 2027?

Nirjala Ekadashi 2027 is on Monday, 14 June.

When is Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2027?

Dev Uthani (Prabodhini) Ekadashi 2027 is on Wednesday, 10 November. Devshayani Ekadashi is on 14 July, so Chaturmas runs from 14 July to 10 November 2027.

What is the difference between Gyaras and Ekadashi?

There is no difference. Ekadashi is called Gyaras in the dialects of North India.

When should the Ekadashi fast be broken?

The fast is broken the next day within Dvadashi tithi, preferably in the morning after worship and charity – not after Dvadashi has ended.
