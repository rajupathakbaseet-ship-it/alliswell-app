Libra Horoscope 2027 (Tula Rashifal 2027): Career Boom, Money Gains, Marriage, Home and Month-by-Month Predictions

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 17 November 2026WfXT
In short: How will 2027 be for Libra (Tula) Moon sign? From 25 January to 25 June exalted Jupiter in the 10th house lifts your career, from 26 June Jupiter in the 11th house increases income, Saturn in the 6th house gives victory over opponents, but Rahu in the 4th house tests peace at home. Full predictions with remedies. हिंदी में पढ़ें

For people with the Moon in Libra (Tula rashi), 2027 is a golden year for career and earnings. On 25 January 2027 Jupiter becomes exalted and enters your 10th house (house of karma) – a very big combination for a high post at work, a name in business and rising respect in society. From 26 June Jupiter moves to the house of gains and opens many routes of income. Saturn stays in the 6th house for most of the year and brings victory over opponents, disease and debt. Only Rahu in the 4th house and Ketu in the 10th house all year will test peace at home and satisfaction at work. Pandit ji says – along with progress outside, give time to your home too; only then will 2027 give its full reward.

Libra 2027 at a Glance

• Best period: 25 January to 25 June 2027 (exalted Jupiter in the 10th house) and 26 June to 26 November (Jupiter in the house of gains)
• Period to be careful: 3 June to 20 October (Saturn in the 7th house – responsibility in relationships) and 5 July to 23 August (Mars in the 12th house)
• Home and family: Rahu in the 4th house – restlessness at home, caution with property papers
• Victory over opponents: Saturn in the 6th house – freedom from cases, competition and disease
• Sade Sati / Dhaiya: in 2027 Libra has neither Sade Sati nor Dhaiya

Now let us see which house of Libra each of these planets occupies and what results it gives.

Career and Job 2027

2027 is very special for Libra careers. From 25 January to 25 June exalted Jupiter stays in the 10th house – promotion, big new responsibility, success in government work and the trust of influential people. Teachers, lawyers, consultants, bankers and managers will gain especially. From 10 March to 25 April Mars is also in the 10th house, adding energy and speed to your work.

With Ketu in the 10th house all year, at times you may lose interest in work or feel that what you get is not enough. Do not quit your job in a hurry then. At the start of the year and after 20 October, Saturn in the 6th house weakens opponents at the office and gives success in competitive exams.

Business 2027

In business, the first part of the year is about name and expansion – Jupiter in the 10th house raises your reputation. From 26 June Jupiter in the house of gains brings big orders, new customers and income through your network. From 3 June to 20 October Saturn stays in the 7th house – effort and responsibility in partnerships increase; put everything with partners in writing. Mars is in the house of wealth from 8 October to 17 November; do not argue with customers then.

Money and Finances 2027

From 26 June to 26 November 2027 Jupiter stays in the house of gains (Leo) – the best financial period of the year. Income from many routes, stuck money returning, and gains from elder siblings or friends. At the start of the year (until 9 March) Mars is also in the house of gains. From 26 November Jupiter moves to the 12th house and expenses will rise, so secure your savings before November. With Rahu in the 4th house, check papers thoroughly when buying property.

Love Life 2027

From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter from the house of gains aspects the 5th house – happiness, understanding and trust in love relationships increase. Those who are single may find a good partner through friends. From 24 August to 7 October Mars stays in your sign – energy and attraction rise, but do not spoil the relationship with anger and stubbornness.

Marriage 2027

For unmarried Libra natives, 26 June to 26 November 2027 is a good time for marriage – Jupiter from the house of gains aspects the 7th house and talks of a relationship move forward. From 3 June to 20 October Saturn in the 7th house makes marriage a little more serious and responsible – relationships formed now tend to be lasting, but there may also be delay. Married people should give time and respect to their spouse during this period and take care of their health. Fix the wedding date only through kundli matching and an auspicious muhurat.

Family and Home 2027

Rahu stays in the 4th house all year – there may be restlessness at home, worry about your mother's health, a wish to change house or a property-related tangle. The good news is that from 25 January to 25 June exalted Jupiter from the 10th house aspects the 4th house and handles troubles at home. From 27 December exalted Mars also joins Rahu in the 4th house – avoid arguments at home and property disputes at the end of the year. Daily puja and lighting a lamp at home will keep peace.

Health 2027

With Saturn in the 6th house you get relief from old illness and stronger resistance. From 3 June Saturn aspects your sign from the 7th house – watch for tiredness, back and joint pain. Mars is in the 12th house from 5 July to 23 August – avoid lack of sleep, eye problems and costly illness. Rahu in the 4th house may cause chest discomfort and mental restlessness – practise meditation and pranayama. For any problem, consult a doctor.

Studies and Exams 2027

2027 is very good for Libra students taking competitive exams – Saturn in the 6th house gives a sure reward for hard work. From 25 January to 25 June Jupiter in the 10th house gives success in government job exams and interviews. After 26 June Jupiter aspects the 5th house and helps you focus on studies. Rahu in the 4th house creates chances of changing your place of study or moving to a hostel.

Libra 2027 Month by Month

January: Mars in the house of gains – good income. Exalted Jupiter in the 10th house from 25 January – a big start in your career.
February: promotion, a new post and support from influential people.
March: Mars in the 10th house from 10 March – energy and speed at work.
April: Jupiter direct from 13 April – stalled plans move ahead. Gains rise from 26 April.
May: a very strong month of the year for career and business.
June: Saturn in the 7th house from 3 June – responsibility in relationships. Jupiter in the house of gains from 26 June – time of earnings.
July: Mars in the 12th house from 5 July – watch expenses and sleep.
August: Saturn retrograde from 9 August. Mars in your sign from 24 August – confidence, but control anger.
September: a good month for money and love.
October: Mars in the house of wealth from 8 October – restraint in speech. Saturn back in the 6th house from 20 October – relief.
November: gains through courage and travel from 18 November. Jupiter in the 12th house from 26 November – expenses rise.
December: Mars and Rahu in the 4th house from 27 December – caution in home and property matters.

Libra 2027: Lucky Numbers, Colours and Days

• Lucky numbers: 6 and 7
• Lucky colours: white, light blue, pink and cream
• Lucky days: Friday and Saturday
• Ruling planet: Venus (Shukra)

Easy Remedies for Libra 2027

1. Worship Goddess Lakshmi every Friday and give white sweets to young girls.
2. For Rahu, donate black sesame or blankets to the poor every Saturday and serve your mother.
3. For Ketu, worship Lord Ganesha and feed rotis to dogs.
4. Donate yellow things on Thursday and take blessings from teachers.
5. Recite the Hanuman Chalisa on Saturday – peace in relationships and work.

Dos and Don'ts in 2027

✅ Do:
• Make every effort for promotion, a new job and business expansion between January and June.
• Save a large part of what you earn from June to November.
• Get all papers checked before buying property.
• Give a little time every day to your family and mother.

❌ Don't:
• Quit your job in a hurry under Ketu's influence.
• Rely on verbal promises in partnerships.
• Let arguments at home and property disputes grow at the end of December.
• Spend unnecessarily or stay up late in July–August.

Pandit ji's Important Note

This horoscope is a general prediction based on the Moon sign and the planetary transits of 2027. Every person's kundli, dasha and antardasha are different, so show your kundli before a big decision. For Libra, 2027 is a year of progress and earnings – balance is the nature of your sign; manage both home and work with that same balance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How will 2027 be for Libra?

2027 is a very good year for Libra career and earnings. From 25 January to 25 June exalted Jupiter in the 10th house lifts your career, and from 26 June to 26 November it increases income from the house of gains. Rahu in the 4th house asks for attention to peace at home.

Is there Sade Sati or Dhaiya on Libra in 2027?

No. In 2027 Saturn stays in Pisces and Aries, which are the 6th and 7th houses from Libra. So Libra has neither Sade Sati nor Dhaiya this year.

When will Libra get a promotion in 2027?

Exalted Jupiter stays in the 10th house from 25 January to 25 June 2027 – the best time for promotion and new responsibility, especially between 10 March and 25 April.

When will Libra get married in 2027?

From 26 June to 26 November 2027 Jupiter from the house of gains aspects the 7th house – a good time for marriage. Get the exact timing fixed through kundli matching and dasha.

When will money come for Libra in 2027?

Jupiter stays in the house of gains from 26 June to 26 November 2027 – the best financial period of the year. Expenses rise after 26 November.

What are the lucky number and colour for Libra in 2027?

Lucky numbers are 6 and 7, lucky colours are white, light blue and pink, and the lucky day is Friday.
