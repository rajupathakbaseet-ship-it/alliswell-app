Shani Sade Sati and Dhaiya 2026-2027: Saturn Turns Direct on 11 December, Enters Aries in 2027 – Who Is Affected and Remedies

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: Saturn turns direct on 11 December 2026, enters Aries on 3 June 2027, returns to Pisces on 20 October and enters Aries for good on 23 February 2028. Here is who has Sade Sati and Dhaiya, and what to do. हिंदी में पढ़ें

The word "Sade Sati" scares many people. But the truth is that Saturn is not a punisher – he is the planet of justice. Those who work honestly often see their biggest success during Sade Sati. Instead of fearing it, understand where Saturn is, when he moves and what you need to do.

Where Is Saturn Now?

Saturn is currently retrograde in Pisces, in Uttara Bhadrapada nakshatra. On 11 December 2026 Saturn turns direct, and work that was stuck during the retrograde starts moving again.

Who Has Sade Sati and Dhaiya Now

Sade Sati: Aquarius – third and last phase; Pisces – second phase (the middle, considered the heaviest); Aries – first phase.
Dhaiya: Leo – Ashtam (8th) Dhaiya; Sagittarius – 4th Dhaiya, also called Kantak Shani.

Saturn's Sign Changes in 2027-2028

  • 3 June 2027 – Saturn moves from Pisces to Aries
  • 20 October 2027 – returns to Pisces while retrograde
  • 23 February 2028 – enters Aries again, now for the long term

So 2027 is a "half here, half there" year. Signs whose Sade Sati is ending will feel full relief only after February 2028.

What Changes When Saturn Enters Aries

  • Sade Sati ends: for Aquarius (full relief from February 2028).
  • Sade Sati begins: the first phase starts for Taurus.
  • Phases change: Pisces enters the last phase, Aries the middle phase.
  • Dhaiya: ends for Leo and Sagittarius; Ashtam Dhaiya begins for Virgo and Kantak (4th) Dhaiya for Capricorn.

The Three Phases of Sade Sati – in Simple Words

  • First phase (Saturn 12th from the Moon): expenses rise, sleep falls, travel or a change of home is likely.
  • Second phase (Saturn on the Moon sign): pressure on the mind, tests of health and relationships – the time we learn the most.
  • Third phase (Saturn 2nd from the Moon): family and money settle, and hard work starts paying off.

People for whom Saturn rules good houses (for example Taurus, Libra, Capricorn or Aquarius ascendants), or whose Saturn is exalted or in its own sign, often gain land, position and lasting success during Sade Sati. So do not fear by sign alone – get your horoscope seen.

A Message for Each Affected Sign

♒ Aquarius – dawn after the dark night: your last phase is running. The fruit of years of struggle will slowly come. Be careful with money and family, keep your words sweet. Relief starts from June 2027, and Sade Sati lifts fully from February 2028.

♓ Pisces – the biggest test, the biggest lesson: Saturn is on your own sign. You may feel a heavy mind, fatigue and distance in relationships. Waking early, working with discipline and stopping wasteful spending will take you far. Stuck work moves after 11 December.

♈ Aries – it is only the beginning: the first phase raises expenses and running around. In June 2027 Saturn comes to your sign and the middle phase begins. Build health and saving habits now; foreign and distant work also brings chances.

♌ Leo – Ashtam Dhaiya: sudden expenses, health trouble or obstacles are possible. Drive carefully and do not trust blindly. It begins to lift when Saturn enters Aries in June 2027.

♐ Sagittarius – Kantak Shani: worries about home, mother, land or vehicles. Keep peace at home; relief after June 2027.

♉ Taurus – time to prepare: your Sade Sati starts from June 2027. Venus, your ruler, is Saturn's friend, so it is often not as hard for you. Still, reduce debt and increase savings now.

♍ Virgo and ♑ Capricorn – Dhaiya from June 2027: Ashtam Dhaiya for Virgo and 4th Dhaiya for Capricorn. Do not rush decisions about health, home and jobs.

The Most Effective Saturn Remedies

  • A mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree on Saturday evenings.
  • Daily Hanuman Chalisa – it is believed Shani Dev does not trouble Hanuman's devotees.
  • Chant 'Om Sham Shanaishcharaya Namah' 108 times.
  • Donate black sesame, urad, mustard oil and blankets to those in need.
  • Treat workers, elders and helpers well – this pleases Saturn the most.
  • Never wear Neelam (blue sapphire) without getting your horoscope checked.

🙏 Pandit Raju Pathak's Advice

Pandit Raju Pathak says – "Saturn does not ask for fear, he asks for discipline. Wake early, keep your word, pay your workers on time and serve the elderly – I have seen people rise during Sade Sati because they did exactly this."

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it fine to marry or buy a house during Sade Sati?

Yes, if the dasha and transits in your horoscope support it. There is no need to stop auspicious work only out of fear of Sade Sati.

Is Sade Sati seen from the Moon sign or the name sign?

The correct calculation is from the Moon sign at birth; the name sign only gives an estimate.

Does everyone suffer in Sade Sati?

No. Many people get jobs, homes and recognition during it. The result depends on your horoscope and your karma.
