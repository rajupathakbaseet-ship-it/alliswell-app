Sagittarius Horoscope 2027 (Dhanu Rashifal 2027): Relief from Shani Dhaiya, Luck, Career, Money, Marriage and Month-by-Month Predictions

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 17 November 2026WfXT
In short: How will 2027 be for Sagittarius (Dhanu) Moon sign? Relief from Saturn's Dhaiya from 3 June, Jupiter in the house of fortune from 26 June to 26 November, and in the 10th house from 26 November to lift your career. Rahu in the 2nd house asks for care with money and speech. Full predictions with remedies. हिंदी में पढ़ें

For people with the Moon in Sagittarius (Dhanu rashi), 2027 is a year of relief and the return of luck. Since March 2025 Saturn has been moving through your 4th house, giving the Dhaiya – unrest at home, worry about your mother's health, property tangles and a heavy mind came from this. On 3 June 2027 Saturn enters Aries, your 5th house, and you get relief from the Dhaiya. From 26 June Jupiter, the lord of your sign, enters the house of fortune and opens the door of luck. Rahu stays in the house of wealth all year, so caution is needed with both money and speech. Pandit ji says – patience until June, progress after that.

Sagittarius 2027 at a Glance

• Best period: 26 June to 26 November 2027 (Jupiter in the house of fortune) and from 26 November (Jupiter in the 10th house)
• Period to be careful: 25 January to 25 June (Jupiter in the 8th house) and 10 March to 25 April (Mars in the 8th house)
• Money and speech: Rahu in the house of wealth all year – ups and downs in money, caution in speech
• Hidden gains: Ketu in the 8th house – interest in spirituality, research and astrology
• Dhaiya: relief on 3 June 2027; the last part runs again from 20 October to 22 February 2028

Now let us see which house of Sagittarius each of these planets occupies and what results it gives.

Sagittarius Shani Dhaiya 2027 – When Will Relief Come

When Saturn is in the 4th house from the Moon sign, it is called Kantak or Chaturth Dhaiya. For Sagittarius it has been running since 29 March 2025. According to the panchang, Saturn moves into Aries on 3 June 2027 and you get relief from the Dhaiya. Saturn turns retrograde on 9 August and returns to Pisces on 20 October, so the last part of the Dhaiya will be felt again from 20 October to 22 February 2028. The Dhaiya ends completely on 23 February 2028. During this time be careful in matters of home, mother, vehicles and property.

Career and Job 2027

At the start of the year Jupiter and Mars are in the house of fortune – support from influential people and gains through travel. From 25 January to 25 June Jupiter stays in the 8th house – sudden changes at work, a change of department or some confidential responsibility may come. Do not rush into changing jobs during this time. From 5 July to 23 August Mars in the 10th house brings speed and authority at work.

From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter in the house of fortune opens paths of progress, and from 26 November Jupiter enters the 10th house (Virgo), giving promotion, a new post and stability in your career. The second half of the year is very good for teachers, consultants and people connected with religion, law and management.

Business 2027

In business, the first part of the year calls for caution – with Jupiter in the 8th house there can be sudden losses through a partner or customer, so keep accounts firm. From 24 August to 7 October Mars in the house of gains gives good profit. After 26 June luck supports you, and work with distant customers, foreign countries or religion-education related fields grows.

Money and Finances 2027

Rahu stays in the house of wealth all year – money will come and may also go suddenly. So keep savings in a separate account and stay away from get-rich-quick schemes. From 25 January to 25 June Jupiter from the 8th house aspects the house of wealth – gains may come through in-laws, insurance or ancestral property. From 24 August to 7 October Mars in the house of gains gives a very good time for earnings. From 27 December Mars also joins Rahu in the house of wealth – be very careful with money transactions at the end of the year.

Love Life 2027

From 3 June to 20 October Saturn stays in the 5th house – seriousness comes into love, but at times there will be distance too. From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter from the house of fortune aspects the 5th house – sincere relationships get support and possibly family approval. With Rahu in the house of wealth, harsh words can spoil relationships – speak sweetly.

Marriage 2027

For unmarried Sagittarius natives, 26 June to 26 November 2027 is a good time for marriage – Jupiter from the house of fortune aspects your sign and creates chances of a proposal from a good family. From 25 January to 25 June, with Jupiter in the 8th house, there may be tension over some matter related to in-laws; be patient. Married people should respect their spouse's family and talk openly about money matters.

Family and Home 2027

Because of the Dhaiya, there may be unrest at home or worry about your mother's health in the first five months of the year. After 3 June the atmosphere at home improves. Rahu in the house of wealth can blow small family matters out of proportion – do not carry one person's words to another. From 26 November Jupiter from the 10th house aspects the 4th house – growth in home, vehicle and happiness from mother.

Health 2027

From 25 January to 25 June Jupiter is in the 8th house, and Ketu is in the 8th house too – pay attention to the stomach, liver, sugar or old illness. From 10 March to 25 April Mars is also in the 8th house – drive very carefully then and do not be careless about injuries or surgery-like situations. Rahu in the house of wealth asks for attention to teeth, throat and eating habits. From 18 November to 26 December Mars in your sign may bring fever and anger. Keep getting regular check-ups.

Studies and Exams 2027

Ketu in the 8th house increases interest in astrology, research, medicine and deep subjects. From 3 June Saturn in the 5th house demands hard work in studies – shortcuts will not work. From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter in the house of fortune creates good chances for higher education, studying abroad and scholarships.

Sagittarius 2027 Month by Month

January: Jupiter and Mars in the house of fortune – travel and support of elders. Jupiter in the 8th house from 25 January – caution.
February: attention to home and your mother's health; control expenses.
March: Mars in the 8th house from 10 March – great care with vehicles and health.
April: Jupiter direct from 13 April – stalled work moves ahead. Luck supports you from 26 April.
May: gains in matters related to in-laws or ancestral property.
June: relief from Dhaiya from 3 June. Jupiter in the house of fortune from 26 June – the best change of the year.
July: Mars in the 10th house from 5 July – speed in your career.
August: Saturn retrograde from 9 August. Mars in the house of gains from 24 August – an excellent time for earnings.
September: a very good month for marriage, travel and financial gain.
October: Mars in the 12th house from 8 October – control expenses. Saturn back in the 4th house from 20 October.
November: Mars in your sign from 18 November – energy, but control anger. Jupiter in the 10th house from 26 November – career progress.
December: Mars and Rahu in the house of wealth from 27 December – great care with money and speech.

Sagittarius 2027: Lucky Numbers, Colours and Days

• Lucky numbers: 3 and 9
• Lucky colours: yellow, saffron and golden
• Lucky days: Thursday and Sunday
• Ruling planet: Jupiter (Guru)

Easy Remedies for Sagittarius 2027

1. Worship Lord Vishnu every Thursday, offer water to a banana tree and chant "Om Brim Brihaspataye Namah".
2. For the Dhaiya, recite the Hanuman Chalisa every Saturday and light a mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree.
3. For Rahu, donate black sesame or blankets to the poor on Saturday and avoid telling lies.
4. For Ketu, offer durva grass to Lord Ganesha.
5. Start the day by touching your mother's feet – the effect of the Dhaiya reduces.

Dos and Don'ts in 2027

✅ Do:
• Take decisions about a new job, marriage and big plans after June.
• Keep savings in a separate account and write down expenses.
• Get regular health check-ups and keep your diet simple.
• Go on religious trips and serve your teachers.

❌ Don't:
• Change job or business in a hurry between January and June.
• Put money into unknown schemes on someone's advice.
• Drive fast or take risky jobs in March–April.
• Speak harshly in the family or pass on gossip.

Pandit ji's Important Note

This horoscope is a general prediction based on the Moon sign and the planetary transits of 2027. Every person's kundli, dasha and antardasha are different, so show your kundli before a big decision. For Sagittarius, 2027 is a year of coming out of a hard time and the return of luck – patience and dharma will carry you forward.

When will money come for Sagittarius in 2027?

From 24 August to 7 October 2027 Mars in the house of gains gives the best time for earnings. With Rahu in the house of wealth, focus on saving.
