Tulsi Vivah 2026 on 21 November: Date, Auspicious Time, Puja Vidhi, Samagri List, Story and Benefits

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: Tulsi Vivah 2026 is on Saturday, 21 November (Kartik Shukla Dwadashi). Perform it in the evening Pradosh Kaal. Some families do it on the evening of 20 November – both traditions are valid. हिंदी में पढ़ें

In Hindu homes tulsi is not just a plant – she is treated like a daughter of the house. The day after Dev Uthani Ekadashi, when Lord Vishnu wakes from his sleep, his Shaligram form is married to Mother Tulsi. Couples who perform Tulsi's kanyadaan are believed to earn the same merit as giving away their own daughter in marriage, and with this wedding the marriage season begins across India.

When is Tulsi Vivah 2026?

  • Date: Saturday, 21 November 2026
  • Tithi: Kartik Shukla Dwadashi (from about 6:32 AM)
  • Nakshatra: Uttara Bhadrapada; Yoga: Siddhi
  • Sunset: about 5:25 PM (Delhi)

The wedding is best performed in the evening Pradosh Kaal – within about two and a half hours after sunset. Some families perform it on the evening of Dev Uthani Ekadashi (20 November), and in many places the celebration continues until Kartik Purnima. Follow your family tradition.

The Story Behind Tulsi Vivah

The Puranas tell of the mighty demon Jalandhar, whose wife Vrinda was a devoted follower of Vishnu. Her chastity made Jalandhar invincible. To protect the gods, Vishnu took Jalandhar's form and broke Vrinda's vow, and Jalandhar was killed. Learning the truth, Vrinda cursed Vishnu to become a stone – the Shaligram. Where her ashes fell, the tulsi plant grew, and Vishnu blessed her: no worship of his would be complete without tulsi, and every year he would marry her in his Shaligram form.

Samagri (Things You Need)

  • A healthy tulsi plant in a pot
  • Shaligram, or a small idol of Vishnu/Krishna
  • 4 sugarcane sticks for the mandap
  • Red chunri or cloth and suhag items (bangles, bindi, sindoor, mehndi, kajal)
  • Yellow cloth and janeu for the Lord
  • Turmeric, kumkum, rice, flowers and garland
  • Seasonal fruit – water chestnut, amla, ber, sweet potato, custard apple
  • Sweets, panchamrit, ghee lamp, incense, camphor, mango leaves and a kalash

Tulsi Vivah Vidhi – Simple Steps

  • Place the tulsi pot in the courtyard or on the roof and decorate it with rangoli.
  • Stand four sugarcane sticks around the pot and tie them at the top – this is the mandap.
  • Dress Tulsi like a bride with the red chunri and suhag items.
  • Bathe the Shaligram with panchamrit, dress it in yellow and seat it to Tulsi's right.
  • Remember Ganesh, worship the kalash, and offer turmeric, kumkum, rice and flowers to both.
  • A male family member carries the Shaligram around Tulsi seven times while wedding songs are sung.
  • The head of the family (or a couple) performs Tulsi's kanyadaan.
  • Do aarti, offer fruit and sweets, and share the prasad. Chant 'Om Tulasyai Namah'.

Benefits and Remedies for Delayed Marriage

Tulsi Vivah is believed to bring the merit of kanyadaan, love and harmony in married life, and the blessings of Goddess Lakshmi. Unmarried girls can offer a red chunri and suhag items to Tulsi; unmarried boys can offer yellow flowers and chana dal to the Shaligram; and anyone can light a ghee lamp near tulsi and walk around it 11 times. Also get the 7th house and Jupiter-Venus in your horoscope checked – the real reason for delay is usually found there.

Tulsi Rules to Remember

  • Do not pluck tulsi on Sundays, Ekadashi or after sunset.
  • Water tulsi every morning and light a lamp in the evening.
  • Do not throw away dried tulsi – immerse it in a river or bury it in the pot's soil.
  • Tulsi is not offered to Lord Ganesh or Lord Shiva.

🙏 Pandit Raju Pathak's Advice

Pandit Raju Pathak says – "Do Tulsi Vivah with the feeling of marrying off your own daughter. If someone in your family is facing delays in marriage, let them take part in the pheras and offer a red chunri to Mother Tulsi with a sincere prayer."

Frequently Asked Questions

Should Tulsi Vivah be done on 20 or 21 November?

Dwadashi begins on the morning of 21 November, so most panchangs give 21 November. Some families do it on the evening of Ekadashi (20 November) – both are accepted.

What if I do not have a Shaligram?

A small idol or picture of Lord Vishnu or Krishna can be used.

Do weddings start after Tulsi Vivah?

Yes. This year the first marriage muhurat is also on 21 November.
