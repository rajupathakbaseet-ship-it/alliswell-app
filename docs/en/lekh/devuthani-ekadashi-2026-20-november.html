Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026 on 20 November: Date, Puja Vidhi, Waking the Gods, Parana Time and Remedies for 12 Signs

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: Dev Uthani (Prabodhini) Ekadashi 2026 falls on Friday, 20 November for householders and Saturday, 21 November for Vaishnavas. Chaturmas ends and auspicious ceremonies begin again. Break the fast on 21 November after sunrise. हिंदी में पढ़ें

Four months ago, on Devshayani Ekadashi (25 July 2026), Lord Vishnu went into yoga-nidra in the cosmic ocean, and weddings, housewarmings and mundan ceremonies were put on hold. On Kartik Shukla Ekadashi he wakes up – that is why this day is called Dev Uthani, Devotthan or Prabodhini Ekadashi. It is counted among the greatest Ekadashis of the year.

When is Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026?

  • Smarta (householders): Friday, 20 November 2026
  • Vaishnava: Saturday, 21 November 2026
  • Ekadashi tithi: from shortly after sunrise on 20 November until about 6:32 AM on 21 November
  • Sunrise (20 Nov): about 6:47 AM, sunset: about 5:25 PM (Delhi)
  • Rahu Kaal (20 Nov): 10:46 AM to 12:06 PM – do not begin the puja in this window

Since the Ekadashi tithi covers almost the whole of 20 November, householders fast on this day; those initiated into the Vaishnava tradition fast on 21 November.

When to Break the Fast (Parana)

If you fast on 20 November, break it on the morning of 21 November after sunrise, during Dwadashi. Offer tulsi leaves and food to Lord Vishnu, feed a Brahmin or a needy person, and then eat yourself. Check the exact parana time for your city in the local panchang.

Why This Ekadashi Matters

It is believed that while Lord Vishnu sleeps, Lord Shiva runs the universe; on Dev Uthani Ekadashi Vishnu takes the responsibility back, so auspicious ceremonies begin again. The Padma Purana says this fast gives the merit of thousands of yagnas. Bhishma Panchak also starts on this day and runs until Kartik Purnima (24 November).

The Tradition of "Waking the Gods"

In villages across North India, women draw the Lord's footprints and a chowk with red ochre or lime in the courtyard in the evening. Sugarcane, water chestnut, sweet potato, ber, radish, amla and new grain are placed on it and covered with a basket. Then the women beat a plate, ring a bell and sing "Utho Dev, baitho Dev…", lift the basket, do aarti and light lamps in every corner of the house.

Puja Vidhi – Step by Step

  • Wake early, bathe, wear clean (preferably yellow) clothes and take the vow of the fast.
  • Bathe Lord Vishnu or Shaligram with panchamrit and offer yellow clothes and flowers.
  • Vishnu puja is incomplete without tulsi – pluck the leaves a day earlier, because tulsi is not plucked on Ekadashi.
  • Chant 'Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya' 108 times; reciting the Vishnu Sahasranama is best.
  • After sunset, perform the waking ritual, light a ghee lamp and do aarti; spend the night in bhajans if you can.

What to Eat and What to Avoid

Allowed: fruit, milk, curd, sabudana, water-chestnut flour, kuttu, potato, sweet potato, makhana, dry fruits and rock salt. Avoid: rice, wheat, lentils, onion, garlic, regular salt and tamasic food. Rice is especially forbidden on Ekadashi. The sick, the elderly and pregnant women can keep a fruit-only fast – never force your health.

Remedies for the 12 Signs

  • Aries: offer jaggery and red flowers to Vishnu.
  • Taurus: worship Vishnu with Lakshmi and offer kheer.
  • Gemini: recite the Vishnu Sahasranama, feed green fodder to a cow.
  • Cancer: bathe Shaligram with milk; donate rice the next day.
  • Leo: offer water to the Sun, donate wheat.
  • Virgo: light a ghee lamp near tulsi and walk around it 7 times.
  • Libra: offer perfume and a white sweet.
  • Scorpio: worship Hanuman along with Vishnu.
  • Sagittarius: wear yellow; donate chana dal and turmeric.
  • Capricorn: give a blanket or warm clothes to someone in need.
  • Aquarius: offer water to a peepal tree and light a lamp.
  • Pisces: worship a banana tree and chant 'Om Namo Narayanaya'.

🙏 Pandit Raju Pathak's Advice

Pandit Raju Pathak says – "Even if you cannot fast, do not eat rice on this day, worship Lord Vishnu and light lamps in the evening. Pluck tulsi leaves a day before. This is the day the season of auspicious work reopens, so start it with gratitude."

Frequently Asked Questions

Can weddings take place on Dev Uthani Ekadashi?

Many places treat it as an 'abujh' (self-auspicious) day. By panchang calculation the first marriage muhurat this season is on 21 November.

When is Tulsi Vivah 2026?

On Saturday, 21 November 2026.

What if I cannot keep the fast?

Avoid rice, worship Lord Vishnu and light lamps in the evening.
