Taurus Horoscope 2027 (Vrishabh Rashifal 2027): Start of Sade Sati, Career, Money, Marriage and Month-by-Month Predictions

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 17 November 2026WfXT
In short: How will 2027 be for Taurus (Vrishabh) Moon sign? Saturn in the 11th house boosts income in the first half, the first phase of Sade Sati begins on 3 June, and from 26 June Jupiter in the 4th house brings the joy of home and vehicle. Career, money, love, marriage, health and every month – with remedies. हिंदी में पढ़ें

For people with the Moon in Taurus (Vrishabh rashi), 2027 is a year of both earnings and new responsibilities. At the start of the year Saturn sits in your 11th house (house of gains) and brings stuck money back, big income and the reward of past hard work. Then from 3 June 2027 Saturn moves into Aries and begins the first phase of your Sade Sati, which increases expenses and running around. Rahu stays in your house of fortune all year, giving chances connected with long journeys, religion and foreign lands. Pandit ji's simple advice: save a large part of whatever you earn in the first six months – that money will be your support later.

Taurus 2027 at a Glance

• Best period: January to 2 June 2027 (Saturn in the 11th house – income and gains)
• Period to be careful: 3 June to 20 October 2027 (Saturn in the 12th house – expenses, sleep, running around) and 18 November to 26 December (Mars in the 8th house)
• Fortune: Rahu in the 9th house – foreign travel, pilgrimages, new teachers and sudden opportunities
• Home and family: Jupiter in the 4th house from 26 June to 26 November – happiness of home, vehicle and mother
• Sade Sati: first phase starts on 3 June 2027 (relief in between from 20 October to February 2028)

Now let us see which house of Taurus each of these planets occupies and what results it gives.

Taurus Sade Sati 2027 – When It Starts and Its Effects

Many Taurus natives ask when their Sade Sati will begin. According to the panchang, Saturn enters Aries on the morning of 3 June 2027, which is the 12th house from Taurus. Your Sade Sati's first phase begins that same day. Saturn turns retrograde on 9 August and goes back to Pisces on 20 October 2027, so you get some months of relief at the end of the year. From 23 February 2028 Saturn re-enters Aries and the first phase then continues without a break.

In the first phase, expenses usually rise, sleep reduces, long-distance travel happens and many people change city or house. There is nothing to fear – people connected with foreign countries, hospitals, import-export or distant work actually get ahead during this time. Just avoid big spending without thought, lending money and show-off purchases now.

Career and Job 2027

The first five months of the year are very strong for your career. Saturn, the lord of your 10th house, sits in the 11th house of gains – this means promotion, support from senior officials and recognition of past work. Do try for a job change or a new post between January and May.

From 25 January to 25 June Jupiter stays in the 3rd house. This benefits work related to writing and speaking, media, marketing, sales and short travel. Brothers and sisters will support you. After 3 June the workload increases and at times you may feel the effort is more and the credit less. Be patient then, do not argue with your boss and do not decide to quit your job in a hurry. From 24 August to 7 October Mars in the 6th house brings victory over opponents and success in competitive exams.

Business 2027

For traders, January to May is a time of expansion. Old customers will return, and big orders and new partnerships become possible. With Rahu in the 9th house, goods linked to foreign countries, online sales and work related to religion or tourism give special profit. From 8 October to 17 November Mars is in the 7th house – keep accounts with partners clear and sign papers only after reading them.

Money and Finances 2027

Financially, the first half of 2027 is the best. Saturn in the house of gains gives steady income, rent, returns from investment and stuck money. After 3 June Saturn in the 12th house increases expenses – money will go on house repairs, travel, treatment or family work. So create an emergency fund before June. Mars is in the 8th house from 18 November to 26 December; avoid shares, speculation or investing on someone else's advice during this time.

Love Life 2027

From 5 July to 23 August Mars is in the 5th house. Love will be passionate, but quarrels over small things can happen too. Listen carefully to your partner. From 26 November 2027 Jupiter moves into your 5th house (Virgo) – from here love relationships deepen and many will reach marriage. Those who are single may meet a good partner during a religious trip or studies.

Marriage 2027

For unmarried Taurus natives, Jupiter in the 4th house from 26 June to 26 November creates an atmosphere of auspicious events at home, and after 26 November Jupiter from the 5th house aspects the 9th and 11th houses and helps relationships get finalised. So the last part of 2027 and the start of 2028 are good for marriage. Married people should avoid arguments and stubbornness with their spouse from 8 October to 17 November (Mars in the 7th house). Fix the wedding date only from your own kundli and an auspicious muhurat in the panchang.

Family and Home 2027

Ketu stays in your 3rd house all year – courage increases, but some distance or misunderstanding with brothers and sisters is possible. Keep communication clear. From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter in the 4th house creates chances of a new house, land, vehicle or home décor. Your mother's blessings and health will both be fine. This is a good time for puja at home or griha pravesh (house-warming).

Health 2027

Health will be good in the first half of the year. After 3 June Saturn in the 12th house may cause problems related to sleep, eyes, feet and the lower back. Cut down on staying up late, too many sweets and heavy food – Taurus natives gain weight easily. Mars is in the 8th house from 18 November to 26 December; drive slowly and avoid sprains and injuries. For any problem, do consult a doctor.

Studies and Exams 2027

Rahu in the 9th house gives opportunities for higher education and studying abroad. From January to June Jupiter in the 3rd house gives the strength to work hard. 24 August to 7 October is a very good time for competitive exams. From 26 November Jupiter in the 5th house strengthens both interest in studies and memory.

Taurus 2027 Month by Month

January: new ways of earning, old money returns. Jupiter in the 3rd house from 25 January – courage and travel.
February: praise at work, meetings with influential people.
March: Mars in the 3rd house from 10 March – courage increases; finish pending work.
April: Jupiter direct from 13 April – stalled plans move ahead. Some running around at home from 26 April.
May: the strongest financial month of the year – lock in savings and investments.
June: first phase of Sade Sati from 3 June. Jupiter in the 4th house from 26 June – chances of house and vehicle.
July: Mars in the 5th house from 5 July – be patient in matters of love and children.
August: Saturn retrograde from 9 August – old work comes back. Victory over opponents from 24 August.
September: a good month for job and exams.
October: Mars in the 7th house from 8 October – caution in partnerships. Saturn back in the house of gains from 20 October – relief.
November: Jupiter in the 5th house from 26 November – good news in love, children and studies.
December: Mars in the house of fortune from 27 December – travel and new beginnings.

Taurus 2027: Lucky Numbers, Colours and Days

• Lucky numbers: 6 and 5
• Lucky colours: white, cream, light blue and light pink
• Lucky days: Friday and Wednesday
• Ruling planet: Venus (Shukra)

Easy Remedies for Taurus 2027

1. Worship Goddess Lakshmi every Friday and offer white sweets.
2. After 3 June, light a mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree every Saturday and chant "Om Sham Shanaishcharaya Namah".
3. For Rahu, donate blankets or black sesame to the poor every Wednesday or Saturday.
4. Offer water to a banana tree on Thursday – Jupiter's support grows stronger.
5. Take the blessings of your parents and elders every day – this is the greatest remedy.

Dos and Don'ts in 2027

✅ Do:
• Lock in savings and investments between January and May.
• Go on religious trips and serve elders – Rahu in the 9th house rewards this.
• Walk daily and control your diet for good health.
• Plan to buy a house or vehicle between June and November.

❌ Don't:
• Lend money or spend for show after 3 June.
• Put money into unknown schemes on someone's advice.
• Spoil relations with siblings over small things.
• Drive fast or take risky jobs in November–December.

Pandit ji's Important Note

This horoscope is a general prediction based on the Moon sign and the planetary transits of 2027. Every person's kundli, dasha and antardasha are different, so show your kundli before any big decision. For Taurus, 2027 is a year of turning hard work into money – if you save wisely, even the Sade Sati period will pass comfortably.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How will 2027 be for Taurus?

The first part of 2027 is very good for Taurus income and career because Saturn is in the 11th house. The first phase of Sade Sati begins on 3 June and increases expenses, but from 26 June Jupiter in the 4th house gives the happiness of home and vehicle.

When will Taurus Sade Sati start?

According to the panchang, Saturn enters Aries on 3 June 2027 and the first phase of Taurus Sade Sati begins. Saturn returns to Pisces from 20 October 2027 to 22 February 2028; after that Sade Sati continues without a break.

When will Taurus get a job or promotion in 2027?

January to May 2027 is the best time for promotion and a new job. From 24 August to 7 October Mars in the 6th house gives success in competitive exams and interviews.

When will Taurus get married in 2027?

The last part of 2027, especially after 26 November when Jupiter enters the 5th house, is good for love and marriage. Get the exact timing fixed from the 7th house and dasha of your own kundli.

When should Taurus buy a house or vehicle in 2027?

Jupiter stays in the 4th house from 26 June to 26 November 2027 – the best time to buy a house, land or vehicle.

What are the lucky number and colour for Taurus in 2027?

Lucky numbers are 6 and 5, lucky colours are white, cream and light blue, and the lucky day is Friday.
