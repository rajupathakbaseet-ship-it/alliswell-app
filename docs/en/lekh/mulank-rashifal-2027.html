Numerology Horoscope 2027: How Will the Year Be for Mulank 1 to 9 – Career, Money, Love, Health and Remedies

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: The digits of 2027 add up to 2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11, and 1 + 1 = 2. Number 2 is ruled by the Moon, so 2027 is a year of emotions, family, partnership and relationships. It is good for Mulank 1, 2, 3 and 9; mixed for 5, 6 and 7; Mulank 4 and 8 need patience and hard work. हिंदी में पढ़ें

Just as astrology reads your future from your Moon sign, numerology reads it from your date of birth. Add the digits of the day you were born until a single digit remains – that is your Mulank (root number). Here is how 2027 looks for Mulank 1 to 9: career, money, love, health and simple remedies.

How to Find Your Mulank

  • Born on the 7th → Mulank 7
  • Born on the 23rd → 2 + 3 = Mulank 5
  • Born on the 29th → 2 + 9 = 11 → 1 + 1 = Mulank 2

Only the day counts – not the month or the year.

2027 Is Number 2 – the Year of the Moon

2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11, and 1 + 1 = 2. Number 2 is ruled by the Moon, the planet of the mind, mother, emotions, family, the public and partnership. So in 2027:

  • More decisions about relationships, marriage and partnerships.
  • Work done together succeeds more than work done alone.
  • People decide on emotion – keep a cool head for big decisions.
  • Food, dairy, water, hotels, nursing and public-facing businesses grow.

How 2027 treats each Mulank depends on how its ruling planet relates to the Moon: the Sun and Mercury are the Moon's friends; Mars, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn are neutral; and Rahu-Ketu have an eclipse-like relationship with it.

Mulank 1 Horoscope 2027 (born on 1, 10, 19, 28)

Ruling planet: Sun • 2027 outlook: Very good ★★★★☆

The Sun and the Moon are friends, so 2027 is a supportive year for you.

💼 Career: Leadership chances, government work, promotion or a new responsibility are likely. Take your team along instead of running ahead alone – this is a year of partnership.

💰 Money: Income stays steady and help from your father or elders brings gains. Spending on home and family will rise.

❤️ Love: Ego can create distance. Respect your spouse's feelings. For singles, marriage talks may move forward.

🩺 Health: Watch your eyes and headaches; drink plenty of water.

🎨 Lucky colour: Saffron, golden • 🔢 Lucky numbers: 1, 2, 3, 9

🙏 Remedy: Offer water to the Sun every morning from a copper pot and chant 'Om Ghrini Suryaya Namah'.

Mulank 2 Horoscope 2027 (born on 2, 11, 20, 29)
