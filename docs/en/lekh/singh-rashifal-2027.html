Leo Horoscope 2027 (Singh Rashifal 2027): Relief from Shani Dhaiya, Jupiter's Support, Career, Money, Marriage and Month-by-Month Predictions

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 17 November 2026WfXT
In short: Leo (Singh) Horoscope 2027: relief from Saturn's Dhaiya on 3 June, Jupiter in your sign from 26 June, Rahu in the 6th beats rivals. Career, marriage, remedies. हिंदी में पढ़ें

For people with the Moon in Leo (Singh rashi), 2027 is a year of relief and a fresh flight. Since March 2025 Saturn has been moving through your 8th house, giving the Ashtam Dhaiya – obstacles at work, health worries and sudden troubles came from this. On 3 June 2027 Saturn enters Aries, your house of fortune, and you will get big relief. From 26 June Jupiter comes into your own sign and restores respect, health and confidence. Rahu stays in the 6th house all year and gives victory over opponents, debt and disease. Pandit ji says – get through the first part of the year with patience; the second part will carry you forward like a lion.

Leo 2027 at a Glance

• Best period: 26 June to 26 November 2027 (Jupiter in your sign) and 3 June to 20 October (Saturn in the house of fortune)
• Period to be careful: 25 January to 25 June (Jupiter in the 12th house – expenses) and 10 March to 25 April (Mars in the 12th house)
• Victory over opponents: Rahu in the 6th house all year – freedom from cases, competition and debt
• Money: Jupiter in the house of wealth from 26 November – growth in savings and family
• Dhaiya: relief from Saturn's Ashtam Dhaiya on 3 June 2027; the last part runs again from 20 October to 22 February 2028

Now let us see which house of Leo each of these planets occupies and what results it gives.

Leo Shani Dhaiya 2027 – When Will Relief Come

When Saturn is in the 8th house from the Moon sign, it is called Ashtam Dhaiya. For Leo this Dhaiya has been running since 29 March 2025. According to the panchang, Saturn moves into Aries on 3 June 2027; the effect of Dhaiya then lifts and luck begins to support you. But Saturn turns retrograde on 9 August and returns to Pisces on 20 October 2027, so the last part of the Dhaiya will be felt again from 20 October to 22 February 2028. On 23 February 2028 Saturn moves into Aries for good and the Dhaiya ends completely.

During Dhaiya, pay attention to health, vehicles, in-laws and sudden expenses. Do not invest money on anyone's say-so, and stay away from illegal work.

Career and Job 2027

With Rahu in the 6th house your hold at work stays strong. People who plotted against you will become weak. This is a very good combination for those in competitive exams, government jobs, police, army, law and medicine. From January to June Jupiter is in the 12th house – opportunities may come for work abroad, in a big company or in a distant city.

From 3 June Saturn in the house of fortune gives support from senior officials and success in long-term plans. From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter in your sign gives chances of leadership, new responsibility and recognition in society. From 27 December exalted Mars joins Rahu in the 6th house – chances of big wins at work and in legal cases at the end of the year.

Business 2027

In business, the first part of the year calls for careful steps – make big new investments after June. From 3 June luck supports you, old stuck deals get completed and distant customers bring profit. From 24 August to 7 October Mars in the 3rd house increases income through marketing, travel and new contacts. Jupiter from your sign aspects the 7th house, so June to November is a good time for partnerships.

Money and Finances 2027

From 25 January to 25 June Jupiter stays in the 12th house – money will go on religious work, travel, the home or auspicious family events. This spending is not bad, but keep accounts. Mars is in the house of wealth from 5 July to 23 August – control both your words and your spending then. From 26 November Jupiter enters the house of wealth (Virgo) – from here begins a good time of growing savings, gains through family and stable income. Rahu in the 6th house will also help you pay off old debts.

Love Life 2027

From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter from your sign aspects the 5th house – sincere love relationships deepen and for many the relationship reaches family approval. From 18 November to 26 December Mars stays in the 5th house (Sagittarius) – passion will be there, but do not let ego cause arguments. Leo natives have high self-respect – learn to bend a little in relationships too.

Marriage 2027

For unmarried Leo natives, 26 June to 26 November 2027 is a very good time for marriage, because Jupiter sitting in your sign directly aspects the 7th house. For married people, the spouse may get progress during this time and sweetness in the relationship increases. At the start of the year Saturn's Dhaiya may bring some tension related to in-laws – handle it with patience. Fix the wedding date through kundli matching and an auspicious muhurat.

Family and Home 2027

With Ketu in the 12th house, the mind will turn towards religion and spirituality, and pilgrimages are likely. From 8 October to 17 November Mars stays in the 4th house – there may be tension at home, concern about your mother's health or spending on house repairs. Light a lamp every morning and evening for peace at home. After 26 November good news may come in the family.

Health 2027

Because of Dhaiya, old illness or problems of the stomach, back or bones may arise at the start of the year. With Rahu in the 6th house your power to fight disease will be good – treatment works quickly. Mars is in the 12th house from 10 March to 25 April – avoid lack of sleep, eye trouble and injury. After 26 June Jupiter in your sign brings clear improvement in health, but watch your weight. For any problem, see a doctor.

Studies and Exams 2027

2027 is very good for Leo students preparing for competitive exams – Rahu in the 6th house keeps you ahead in competition. For those dreaming of studying abroad, January to June (Jupiter in the 12th house) is a good time. From June to November Jupiter aspects the 5th house and helps you focus on studies.

Leo 2027 Month by Month

January: Mars retrograde in your sign – control anger. Jupiter in the 12th house from 25 January – expenses and travel.
February: victory over opponents, relief in old cases.
March: Mars in the 12th house from 10 March – watch sleep and expenses.
April: Jupiter direct from 13 April. Mars back in your sign from 26 April – courage rises.
May: time for religious travel and making new plans.
June: relief from Dhaiya from 3 June, Saturn in the house of fortune. Jupiter in your sign from 26 June – the biggest change of the year.
July: Mars in the house of wealth from 5 July – control speech and spending.
August: Saturn retrograde from 9 August. Gains through courage and travel from 24 August.
September: a very good month for career, marriage and respect.
October: avoid tension at home from 8 October. Saturn back in the 8th house from 20 October – be careful.
November: passion in love from 18 November. Jupiter in the house of wealth from 26 November – savings grow.
December: exalted Mars in the 6th house from 27 December – big victories over opponents and in court cases.

Leo 2027: Lucky Numbers, Colours and Days

• Lucky numbers: 1 and 9
• Lucky colours: golden, saffron, orange and red
• Lucky days: Sunday and Tuesday
• Ruling planet: Sun (Surya)

Easy Remedies for Leo 2027

1. Every morning offer water to the Sun from a copper pot and say "Om Ghrini Suryaya Namah".
2. For Dhaiya, light a mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree every Saturday and recite the Hanuman Chalisa.
3. Donate yellow things (chana dal, turmeric, bananas) on Thursday.
4. For Ketu, worship Lord Ganesha and feed rotis to dogs.
5. Respect your father and elders – the Sun becomes stronger.

Dos and Don'ts in 2027

✅ Do:
• Take big decisions like a new job, business expansion and marriage after June.
• Put full effort into competitive exams and old legal cases – Rahu is with you.
• Go on religious trips and give charity – 12th-house spending becomes auspicious.
• Get regular health check-ups.

❌ Don't:
• Make big investments or take risks between January and May.
• Spoil relations with anyone out of ego.
• Be careless during the last part of Dhaiya from October to February 2028.
• Stay up late or take intoxicants.

Pandit ji's Important Note

This horoscope is a general prediction based on the Moon sign and the planetary transits of 2027. Every person's kundli, dasha and antardasha are different, so show your kundli before a big decision. For Leo, 2027 is a year of relief and new recognition after a hard time – be patient; after June luck will support you fully.
