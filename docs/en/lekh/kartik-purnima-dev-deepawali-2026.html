Kartik Purnima 2026 on 24 November: Dev Deepawali, Guru Nanak Jayanti, Purnima Tithi Time, Snan-Daan and Deepdaan

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: Kartik Purnima 2026 falls on Tuesday, 24 November. Purnima runs from about 11:42 PM on 23 November to about 8:23 PM on 24 November (Delhi). Dev Deepawali and Guru Nanak Jayanti are on the same day. हिंदी में पढ़ें

Of the twelve full moons in a year, Kartik Purnima is considered the holiest. It is believed that the gods themselves come down to light lamps on the ghats of the Ganga on this night. It is also the Prakash Parv of Guru Nanak Dev Ji. A holy dip, charity, lamps and devotion all on one day – that is why it is also called Tripurari Purnima and Dev Deepawali.

Kartik Purnima 2026 – Date and Tithi

  • Date: Tuesday, 24 November 2026
  • Purnima begins: about 11:42 PM on 23 November 2026
  • Purnima ends: about 8:23 PM on 24 November 2026
  • Nakshatra: Krittika
  • Sunrise: about 6:50 AM, sunset: about 5:24 PM (Delhi)

Since Purnima prevails at sunrise on 24 November, the holy dip, charity and fast are on 24 November. Bhadra is present in the morning, so start any new auspicious work only after it ends; bathing, charity and worship are not affected by Bhadra.

Why Kartik Purnima Is So Special

  • Tripurasur's defeat: Lord Shiva is believed to have destroyed the demon Tripurasur on this day; the gods lit lamps in Kashi in celebration – the origin of Dev Deepawali.
  • Matsya avatar: Lord Vishnu took the fish incarnation on this tithi to save the Vedas during the deluge.
  • End of Kartik month: month-long Kartik vows of morning baths, tulsi puja and lamps conclude today, as does Bhishma Panchak (20–24 November).

Dev Deepawali in Kashi

In Varanasi, lakhs of lamps light up almost every ghat of the Ganga this evening. If you cannot go to Kashi, light lamps near tulsi, in your home temple, kitchen and at the main door – the merit is the same.

Guru Nanak Jayanti (Prakash Parv)

Guru Nanak Dev Ji's birth anniversary is celebrated on Kartik Purnima with Akhand Path, kirtan and langar in gurdwaras. His message – "Naam japo, kirat karo, vand chhako" (remember God, earn honestly, share with others) – is as relevant as ever.

What to Do on Kartik Purnima

  • Bathe before sunrise in a holy river, or at home with a little Gangajal in the water.
  • Offer water, red flowers and rice to the Sun from a copper pot.
  • Listen to or perform the Satyanarayan Katha.
  • Donate grain, jaggery, sesame, blankets, warm clothes and lamps – blankets are especially meritorious as winter begins.
  • Light lamps in the evening Pradosh Kaal at a river, pond, temple or at home; always light a ghee lamp near tulsi.
  • At night, offer water mixed with milk to the Moon.

🙏 Pandit Raju Pathak's Advice

Pandit Raju Pathak says – "On Kartik Purnima, do not let the evening pass without lighting lamps – at least one near tulsi and one at the main door. Donate a blanket to someone who needs it; in this cold season that single act of kindness carries great merit."
