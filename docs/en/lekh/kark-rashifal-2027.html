Cancer Horoscope 2027 (Kark Rashifal 2027): Exalted Jupiter, Career, Money, Marriage and Month-by-Month Predictions

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 17 November 2026WfXT
In short: Cancer (Kark) Horoscope 2027: Jupiter exalted in your sign (25 Jan–25 Jun) raises respect and luck; Rahu in the 7th asks care in relationships. With remedies. हिंदी में पढ़ें

For people with the Moon in Cancer (Kark rashi), 2027 is a very auspicious year that comes after many years. On 25 January 2027 Jupiter enters its sign of exaltation, Cancer – your own sign – and stays here until 25 June. In astrology, exalted Jupiter on the Moon sign is considered a great blessing – it improves respect, health, confidence, children and luck. Saturn too stays in your 9th and 10th houses through the year and strengthens fortune and career. Only Ketu on your sign and Rahu in the 7th house will test relationships and peace of mind. Pandit ji says – this is a year to lay foundations; whatever you start now will bear fruit for many years to come.

Cancer 2027 at a Glance

• Best period: 25 January to 25 June 2027 (exalted Jupiter in your sign)
• Big career period: 3 June to 20 October 2027 (Saturn in the 10th house)
• Period to be careful: 10 March to 25 April (debilitated Mars in your sign) and from 27 December (Mars with Rahu in the 7th house)
• Relationships: Rahu in the 7th house – trust and clear talk with spouse and partners are essential
• Sade Sati / Dhaiya: in 2027 Cancer has neither Sade Sati nor Dhaiya

Now let us see which house of Cancer each of these planets occupies and what results it gives.

Effect of Exalted Jupiter on Cancer

Jupiter is exalted in Cancer, meaning it is in its most powerful state. From 25 January to 25 June 2027 Jupiter stays in your sign and aspects the 5th, 7th and 9th houses. This simply means – happiness from children, success in studies, chances of marriage and the support of luck. People who have been troubled for a long time will find relief and the right path now. By Jupiter's grace you will receive blessings from elders and teachers. Ketu is also in your sign, so at times the mind may feel low or lonely – Jupiter will handle this; just stay connected with prayer and meditation.

Career and Job 2027

At the start of the year Saturn is in the 9th house (house of fortune) – support from senior officials, long journeys and gains in work connected with religion. From 3 June to 20 October Saturn enters the 10th house – the most important time for your career. Work pressure will rise, but so will position, authority and recognition. People in government jobs, administration, politics and large organisations will gain especially. Remember that Saturn does not forgive laziness and dishonesty during this time.

From 18 November to 26 December Mars stays in the 6th house – a very good time for competition, interviews and victory over opponents.

Business 2027

In business, the first part of 2027 is good for expansion – by Jupiter's grace new plans and contacts with influential people will form. But with Rahu in the 7th house, be especially careful in partnerships. Do not blindly trust an unknown or overly sweet-talking partner. Do every deal in writing. From 27 December Mars also joins Rahu in the 7th house – avoid big disputes or court cases at the end of the year.

Money and Finances 2027

From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter stays in your house of wealth (Leo) – a time to increase savings, gain money through family and benefit from old investments. At the start of the year (until 9 March) retrograde Mars is also in the house of wealth; keep expenses under control then. From 26 November Jupiter moves to the 3rd house, and then your own effort and courage will matter more for earning. Overall, 2027 is a balanced and good year financially.

Love Life 2027

Exalted Jupiter aspects the 5th house, so sincere love relationships will grow stronger. From 8 October to 17 November Mars stays in the 5th house (Scorpio) – passion in love increases, but jealousy or anger can spoil the relationship. With Rahu in the 7th house, an attractive person may confuse you – do not rush into a new relationship; first get to know the person well.

Marriage 2027

For unmarried Cancer natives, 25 January to 25 June 2027 is a very strong time for marriage, because exalted Jupiter directly aspects the 7th house. Relationships that have been stuck for a long time can be finalised now. For married people, Rahu in the 7th house can create misunderstandings – talk openly with your spouse and do not let a third person interfere in your relationship. From 3 June to 20 October Saturn also aspects the 7th house, so keep patience and responsibility in the relationship. Fix the wedding date only through kundli matching and an auspicious muhurat.

Family and Home 2027

By Jupiter's grace auspicious events may happen in the family – a wedding, the birth of a child or a religious function. Mars is in the 4th house from 24 August to 7 October, and Saturn aspects the 4th house from June to October – there may be tension at home, concern for your mother's health or spending on house repairs then. Take special care of your mother. Take any big property decision after October.

Health 2027

With exalted Jupiter in your sign, health improves and relief from old illness is possible. But Jupiter's period also carries a risk of weight gain – watch your diet. From 10 March to 25 April debilitated Mars stays in your sign – be careful about anger, fever, blood pressure and injuries then. With Ketu on your sign the mind may be restless at times – meditation, pranayama and good sleep are essential. For any problem, consult a doctor.

Studies and Exams 2027

2027 is a very good year for Cancer students. Exalted Jupiter aspects the 5th house and gives memory, understanding and success in exams. January to June is the best time for board exams, admission to higher education and scholarships. There are chances of success in competitive exams from 18 November to 26 December.

Cancer 2027 Month by Month

January: exalted Jupiter in your sign from 25 January – new energy, confidence and good news.
February: talks of relationships and marriage move ahead; luck supports you.
March: Mars in your sign from 10 March – avoid anger and haste, take care of health.
April: Jupiter direct from 13 April – stuck work gets done. Mars in the house of wealth from 26 April – keep an eye on expenses.
May: a very auspicious month for marriage, children and studies.
June: Saturn in the 10th house from 3 June – big responsibility at work. Jupiter in the house of wealth from 26 June.
July: Mars in the 3rd house from 5 July – courage increases, support from siblings.
August: Saturn retrograde from 9 August. From 24 August avoid tension at home and look after your mother.
September: hard work at the job pays off; savings grow.
October: patience in matters of love and children from 8 October. Saturn back in the house of fortune from 20 October.
November: victory over opponents from 18 November. Jupiter in the 3rd house from 26 November.
December: Mars and Rahu in the 7th house from 27 December – great caution in partnerships and relationships.

Cancer 2027: Lucky Numbers, Colours and Days

• Lucky numbers: 2 and 7
• Lucky colours: white, silver, cream and light yellow
• Lucky days: Monday and Thursday
• Ruling planet: Moon (Chandra)

Easy Remedies for Cancer 2027

1. Every Monday offer water and raw milk on the Shivling and chant "Om Namah Shivaya".
2. Donate yellow things on Thursday and take blessings from elders and teachers.
3. For Rahu, donate black sesame or blankets to the poor on Saturday, and respect your spouse.
4. For Ketu, worship Lord Ganesha and feed rotis to dogs.
5. Start the day by touching your mother's feet – the Moon becomes stronger.

Dos and Don'ts in 2027

✅ Do:
• Take decisions about marriage, studies, new beginnings and big plans between January and June – Jupiter is with you.
• Do not run from responsibility at work – June to October brings a big reward for hard work.
• Do a little meditation and prayer daily – the restlessness from Ketu will go away.
• Put every deal and partnership in writing.

❌ Don't:
• Take any decision in anger in March–April.
• Blindly trust what a third person says about your relationship.
• Eat too much fried and sweet food – weight may increase.
• Get entangled in court cases or big disputes at the end of December.

Pandit ji's Important Note

This horoscope is a general prediction based on the Moon sign and the planetary transits of 2027. Every person's kundli, dasha and antardasha are different, so show your kundli before a big decision. For Cancer, 2027 is a year of Jupiter's grace – recognise this opportunity and begin good work.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How will 2027 be for Cancer?

2027 is a very auspicious year for Cancer. From 25 January to 25 June Jupiter stays exalted in your sign, improving respect, health, marriage prospects and luck. From 3 June to 20 October Saturn in the 10th house gives big career responsibility.

Is there Sade Sati or Dhaiya on Cancer in 2027?

No. In 2027 Saturn stays in Pisces and Aries, which are the 9th and 10th houses from Cancer. So Cancer has neither Sade Sati nor Dhaiya this year.

When will Cancer get married in 2027?

25 January to 25 June 2027 is the strongest time for marriage, because exalted Jupiter directly aspects the 7th house. Get the exact timing fixed through kundli matching and dasha.

When will Cancer get a job or promotion in 2027?

From 3 June to 20 October 2027 Saturn in the 10th house increases position and authority. From 18 November to 26 December Mars in the 6th house brings victory in competition.

When is the Jupiter transit in Cancer in 2027?

According to the panchang, Jupiter enters Cancer on 25 January 2027, turns direct on 13 April and moves to Leo on 26 June 2027.

What are the lucky number and colour for Cancer in 2027?

Lucky numbers are 2 and 7, lucky colours are white, silver and cream, and the lucky day is Monday.
