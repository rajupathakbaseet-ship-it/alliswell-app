Amavasya 2027 Date List: All 13 New Moon Dates, Tithi Timings, Mauni, Somvati and Shani Amavasya

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 25 November 2026WfXT
In short: There are 13 Amavasyas in 2027. The first, Pausha Amavasya, is on Thursday 7 January and the last, again Pausha Amavasya, on Monday 27 December. Mauni Amavasya (also Shani Amavasya) is on 6 February, and Somvati Amavasya falls on 8 March, 2 August and 27 December. Dates are matched with the New Delhi panchang (IST).

Amavasya is the new moon day, the last tithi of the Krishna paksha, when the Sun and Moon are in the same sign and the Moon is not visible. It is the day for remembering ancestors (pitru tarpan), charity and Shiva puja. In everyday speech it is also called Amavas or Mavas.

Special Amavasyas of 2027

Mauni Amavasya – 6 February 2027, Saturday

The Amavasya of Magha is Mauni Amavasya, when people bathe in the Ganga or Sangam while keeping silence. In 2027 it falls on a Saturday, so it is also Shani Amavasya – a good opportunity for Shani remedies for those under Sade Sati or Dhaiya. Read Shani Sade Sati and Dhaiya →

Somvati Amavasya 2027

An Amavasya falling on Monday is called Somvati Amavasya. In 2027 Amavasya falls on Monday on 8 March (Phalguna), 2 August (Shravana) and 27 December (Pausha). Married women circle the peepal tree on this day praying for their husband's long life.

Hariyali Amavasya – 2 August 2027, Monday

The Amavasya of Sawan is Hariyali Amavasya. Planting trees and worshipping Shiva are considered auspicious. In 2027 it is on a Monday, so Somvati and Hariyali Amavasya come together.

Ashwin Amavasya – 29/30 September 2027

Pitru Paksha ends on Ashwin Amavasya. The shraddha of ancestors whose tithi is not known is done on this Amavasya. Check the exact shraddha time with your pandit or local panchang.

Do's and don'ts on Amavasya

• Bathe in a holy river, or at home with a little Gangajal in the water.
• Offer tarpan for your ancestors and give food and charity to a Brahmin or someone in need.
• In the evening light a mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree.
• New ventures, weddings, engagements and big purchases are usually avoided on Amavasya.
• Stay away from meat and alcohol and avoid quarrels.

🙏 Pandit Raju Pathak's Advice

Pandit Raju Pathak says – "Never forget to remember your ancestors on Amavasya. Even if you cannot do a big shraddha or charity, light one mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree in the evening and ask your ancestors for blessings – that is enough. Those under Shani Sade Sati or Dhaiya should donate black sesame and mustard oil on the Shani Amavasya of 6 February 2027."

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When is the next Amavasya?

The next Amavasya is Margashirsha Amavasya on Tuesday, 8 December 2026. The first Amavasya of 2027 is Pausha Amavasya on Thursday, 7 January.

How many Amavasyas are there in 2027?

There are 13 Amavasyas in 2027. Pausha Amavasya comes in both January and December.

When is Mauni Amavasya 2027?

Mauni (Magha) Amavasya 2027 is on Saturday, 6 February. It is also Shani Amavasya.

When is Somvati Amavasya 2027?

In 2027 Amavasya falls on a Monday on 8 March, 2 August and 27 December.

What is Darsha Amavasya?

The day of Amavasya tithi when the Moon is completely invisible and the Amavasya fast is kept is called Darsha Amavasya. Sometimes it falls a day before the bath-charity Amavasya.

Is there a difference between Amavas and Amavasya?

No. Amavasya is also called Amavas or Mavas in everyday speech.
