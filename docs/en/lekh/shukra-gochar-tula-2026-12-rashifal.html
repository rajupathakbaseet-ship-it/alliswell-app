Venus Transit in Libra 2026 (Shukra Gochar Tula): Effects on All 12 Signs from Aries to Pisces

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 22 November 2026WfXT
In short: On 22 November 2026 Venus (Shukra) leaves Virgo and returns to its own sign Libra – at 5:38 PM according to the New Delhi Drik Panchang. Below are general effects for all 12 Moon signs, a mantra and simple remedies.

Venus had gone back into Virgo while retrograde on 6 November and, after turning direct around 14 November, is now re-entering Libra. Timings can differ by a few minutes between panchangs, places and calculation methods; one Lahiri-based calculation gives 5:21 PM IST – so follow your local panchang.

Note from Pandit Raju Pathak: a transit should not be judged alone. The position of Venus in the birth chart, the running dasha and the ascendant can change the result. The signs below are general indications, not fixed predictions.

Why is the Venus transit in Libra special?

In Vedic astrology Venus signifies love, married life, art, attraction, comfort and material pleasures. Libra is Venus's own sign and is linked with balance, partnership and justice. So this transit can draw attention to communication in relationships, working partnerships and work related to beauty or creativity. It does not mean everyone will get the same result.

It is useful to see the big transits of this time in context too. In astrological tradition the Jupiter transit shows growth and learning, the Saturn transit responsibility and patience, and the Rahu-Ketu transit change and priorities. The result of Venus is read in the context of the whole birth chart and Moon sign, not separately from these.

Venus Transit 2026: Effects on All 12 Signs

These are general indications by Moon sign. If you do not know your Moon sign, see your birth chart.

Aries (Mesh)

There may be a chance to make communication in partnership and married life easier. Singles should take time before taking an introduction forward. If there is a new business partnership, keep the terms clear.

Taurus (Vrishabh)

Venus is your sign lord; attention may grow on your work routine, cooperation and health habits. Polite talk will help improve harmony with colleagues. Spend on comfort or shopping according to your budget.

Gemini (Mithun)

There are signs of happiness in creative work, love and plans related to children. People in art, writing or presentation can show their ideas. Keep practising instead of expecting quick gains.

Cancer (Kark)

Attention may go to home, family, decoration and time with loved ones. Plan household purchases or repairs. If there is disagreement over an old matter in the family, a soft tone will help.

Leo (Singh)

New ideas may come through conversations, short trips and contacts. Keep coordination with siblings or neighbours. Do not accept a proposal only on the basis of attractive words; read the details.

Virgo (Kanya)

It is time to review money, savings and your priorities. Keep money talks with the family clear and avoid spending for show. Asking for a fair price for your talent can also be a positive use of this transit.

Libra (Tula)

Venus coming into its own sign emphasises confidence, personality and balance in relationships. State your choices and needs clearly, but also listen to the other side. Set a limit when spending on looks or comfort.

Scorpio (Vrishchik)

Spending some time alone to understand emotional and financial matters can be useful. If old relationships or pending expenses come up again, do not hurry. Look after your sleep and rest routine.

Sagittarius (Dhanu)

You may get support from friends, groups and future plans. If networking creates work opportunities, write down goals and deadlines. Think about your own comfort before lending money to a friend.

Capricorn (Makar)

You may need to pay attention to your image at work, responsibilities and relations with seniors. Present your views creatively and show results. Keeping long-term planning in view will make decisions more balanced.

Aquarius (Kumbh)

Interest in study, travel, guidance and new perspectives may grow. Advice from a teacher or experienced person will be beneficial. Check beliefs related to love or money with facts and good sense.

Pisces (Meen)

Matters of shared money, emotional closeness and trust may come up. Make financial plans openly with your partner. Get full information before any investment or big expense, and ask a qualified financial adviser if needed.

Shukra mantra and simple remedies

On Friday, worship with cleanliness and restraint. If you have faith, offer white flowers and chant this mantra 11 or 108 times: Om Draam Dreem Draum Sah Shukraya Namah.

As remedies, adopt respect in relationships, keeping your word, practising an art and helping the needy as you can. Before wearing a gemstone or doing an expensive remedy, show your kundli to a qualified astrologer. Treat the mantra as a practice of personal faith, not a guaranteed solution to any problem.
