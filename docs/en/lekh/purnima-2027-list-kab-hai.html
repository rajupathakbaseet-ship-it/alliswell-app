Purnima 2027 Date List: All 12 Full Moon Dates, Tithi Timings, Guru Purnima, Buddha Purnima and Kartik Purnima

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 25 November 2026WfXT
In short: There are 12 Purnimas in 2027. The first, Pausha Purnima, is on Friday 22 January and the last, Margashirsha Purnima, is on Monday 13 December. Buddha Purnima is on 20 May, Guru Purnima on 18 July and Kartik Purnima on 14 November (fast on 13 November). Dates are matched with the New Delhi panchang (IST).

Purnima, the full moon day, is the last tithi of the Shukla paksha. On this day the Moon is at its full strength, and bathing in holy rivers, charity, the Satyanarayan katha and offering water (arghya) to the Moon are traditional. Purnima is also called Poonam, Purnamasi and Paurnima.

Special Purnimas of 2027

Magha Purnima – 20 February 2027, Saturday

Bathing in the Ganga, Yamuna and Sangam on Magha Purnima is very important; it is a main bathing day of the Magh Mela at Prayagraj. Donating sesame, blankets and grain is considered meritorious.

Buddha Purnima – 20 May 2027, Thursday

Vaishakha Purnima is regarded as the day of Lord Buddha's birth, enlightenment and mahaparinirvana. In Hindu tradition too it is auspicious for bathing, charity and Vishnu puja.

Guru Purnima – 18 July 2027, Sunday

Ashadha Purnima is called Guru Purnima or Vyasa Purnima, the birth day of Maharshi Ved Vyasa. People seek the blessings of their guru, parents and teachers.

Shravana Purnima – 16/17 August 2027

The Purnima of Sawan is special for Lord Shiva's worship. In 2027 the Purnima fast is on 16 August (Monday) and Purnima on 17 August (Tuesday).

Kartik Purnima – 13/14 November 2027

Dev Deepawali is celebrated on Kartik Purnima and a holy dip in the Ganga has great importance. In 2027 the fast is on 13 November (Saturday) and Kartik Purnima on 14 November (Sunday).

How to keep the Purnima fast

• Bathe in the morning and worship Lord Vishnu or Satyanarayan; listen to the Satyanarayan katha if possible.
• Keep a fruit fast during the day and break it in the evening after offering arghya to the Moon.
• Offer the Moon water mixed with milk and white flowers, saying "Om Somaya Namah".
• Donate white things – rice, milk, sugar, cloth.
• The elderly, the sick and pregnant women should fast according to their health.

Astrological importance of Purnima

On Purnima the Moon is at full strength. In astrology the Moon signifies the mind, the mother and emotions. People whose Moon is weak in the kundli, whose mind is restless or who do not sleep well are advised to keep the Purnima fast and offer arghya to the Moon. Emotions run high on this day, so take big decisions calmly.
