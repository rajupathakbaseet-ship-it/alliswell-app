Scorpio Horoscope 2027 (Vrishchik Rashifal 2027): Support of Luck, Career, Money Gains, Marriage and Month-by-Month Predictions

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 17 November 2026WfXT
In short: Scorpio (Vrishchik) Horoscope 2027: exalted Jupiter in the 9th (25 Jan–25 Jun) brightens luck, Rahu in the 3rd raises courage and income. With remedies. हिंदी में पढ़ें

For people with the Moon in Scorpio (Vrishchik rashi), 2027 is a year of luck and courage. On 25 January 2027 Jupiter becomes exalted and enters your 9th house (house of fortune) – the kind of combination in which stalled work starts getting done by itself, the blessings of elders and teachers come, and the mind turns to religion and good deeds. Rahu stays in the 3rd house all year, making you fearless and increasing income through your own effort. From 3 June to 20 October Saturn in the 6th house gives victory over opponents and disease. Only at the start of the year and after October, Saturn in the 5th house asks for attention to children and studies. Pandit ji says – in 2027 opportunities will knock at your door; just recognise them and move ahead with courage.

Scorpio 2027 at a Glance

• Best period: 25 January to 25 June 2027 (exalted Jupiter in the house of fortune) and 3 June to 20 October (Saturn in the 6th house)
• Career: Jupiter in the 10th house from 26 June to 26 November – position and prestige
• Period to be careful: 24 August to 7 October (Mars in the 12th house) and the months when Saturn is in the 5th house (children and studies)
• Courage and income: Rahu in the 3rd house all year – success in media, marketing, travel and on your own strength
• Sade Sati / Dhaiya: in 2027 Scorpio has neither Sade Sati nor Dhaiya

Now let us see which house of Scorpio each of these planets occupies and what results it gives.

Career and Job 2027

At the start of the year (until 24 January) both Jupiter and Mars are in the 10th house – speed and responsibility at work. From 25 January Jupiter in the house of fortune gives progress through long journeys, foreign lands, government work and support from senior officials. From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter is again in the 10th house – the best time for promotion, a new post and recognition in society.

From 3 June to 20 October Saturn stays in the 6th house – opponents at the office weaken, and you get success in competitive exams and job interviews. With Rahu in the 3rd house, you get a special edge in media, YouTube, writing, marketing, sales and technical work. From 8 October to 17 November Mars stays in its own sign with you – energy and leadership power increase.

Business 2027

In business, 2027 is a year to move ahead with courage. Rahu in the 3rd house gives income through new experiments, online sales and short trips. With Jupiter in the house of fortune, old stuck deals get completed. From 5 July to 23 August Mars in the house of gains can give big profit. From 26 November Jupiter enters the house of gains and makes the end of the year profitable too.

Money and Finances 2027

Financially, 2027 is a good year. Jupiter in the house of fortune brings sudden gains and stuck money. 5 July to 23 August and the period after 26 November are especially good for earnings. Mars is in the 12th house from 24 August to 7 October – avoid unnecessary spending and lending then. Mars is in the house of wealth from 18 November to 26 December; do not let harsh words cause financial loss.

Love Life 2027

At the start of the year and after 20 October Saturn stays in the 5th house (house of love) – seriousness comes into love, but at times there may be distance or coldness. From 25 January to 25 June exalted Jupiter from the house of fortune aspects the 5th house and supports the relationship. Sincere relationships may get the approval of elders during this time. Avoid the habit of jealousy and suspicion with your partner – Scorpio relationships often go wrong because of this.

Marriage 2027

For unmarried Scorpio natives, 25 January to 25 June 2027 is auspicious – Jupiter from the house of fortune aspects your sign and creates chances of a proposal from a good family. From 26 November Jupiter from the house of gains aspects the 7th house – the end of the year is also good for finalising a marriage. For married people 2027 is balanced; just avoid needless arguments from 24 August to 7 October.

Family and Home 2027

Ketu stays in the 9th house all year – there may be differences of opinion with your father or worry about his health. Respect him and look after his health. The mind will turn to religion and pilgrimage. From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter from the 10th house aspects the 4th house – chances of happiness at home, a new vehicle or home décor. Saturn in the 5th house asks for attention to your children's studies and health.

Health 2027

From 3 June to 20 October Saturn in the 6th house gives relief from old illness. Mars is in the 12th house from 24 August to 7 October – avoid lack of sleep, burning eyes and injury. From 8 October to 17 November Mars in your sign may bring anger, blood pressure and fever – keep a cool head. Exercise daily and drink more water. For any problem, see a doctor.

Studies and Exams 2027

With Saturn in the 5th house you will need to work harder at studies, but whatever you study will be firmly remembered. For higher education, research and studying abroad, 25 January to 25 June (Jupiter in the house of fortune) is the best time. There are very good chances of success in competitive exams from 3 June to 20 October.

Scorpio 2027 Month by Month

January: Jupiter and Mars in the 10th house – speed at work. Exalted Jupiter in the house of fortune from 25 January – support of luck.
February: long journeys, religious work and blessings of elders.
March: Mars in the house of fortune from 10 March – do not argue with your father.
April: Jupiter direct from 13 April – stuck work gets done. Mars in the 10th house from 26 April – energy in your career.
May: a good month for higher education, foreign matters and marriage talks.
June: Saturn in the 6th house from 3 June – victory over opponents. Jupiter in the 10th house from 26 June – chances of promotion.
July: Mars in the house of gains from 5 July – a very good time for earnings.
August: Saturn retrograde from 9 August. Mars in the 12th house from 24 August – watch expenses and sleep.
September: recognition at work, but keep expenses under control.
October: Mars in your sign from 8 October – energy, but control anger. Saturn back in the 5th house from 20 October.
November: Mars in the house of wealth from 18 November – restraint in speech. Jupiter in the house of gains from 26 November – income grows.
December: exalted Mars joins Rahu in the 3rd house from 27 December – great courage and success.

Scorpio 2027: Lucky Numbers, Colours and Days

• Lucky numbers: 9 and 3
• Lucky colours: red, maroon and saffron
• Lucky days: Tuesday and Thursday
• Ruling planet: Mars (Mangal)

Easy Remedies for Scorpio 2027

1. Every Tuesday offer sindoor and jasmine oil to Hanuman ji and recite the Hanuman Chalisa.
2. For Ketu, worship Lord Ganesha and take your father's blessings daily.
3. For Saturn, light a mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree on Saturday, especially at the start of the year and after October.
4. Donate yellow things on Thursday and do service at a temple.
5. Help your brothers and sisters – Rahu in the 3rd house rewards this well.

Dos and Don'ts in 2027

✅ Do:
• Go on pilgrimage, pursue higher education and take big decisions from January to June.
• Step forward for promotion and new responsibility between June and November.
• Take your skills to the world through social media or marketing.
• Give time to your children and keep an eye on their studies.

❌ Don't:
• Argue with your father or teachers.
• Spend unnecessarily or lend money in August–October.
• Give place to suspicion and jealousy in relationships.
• Take a big decision in anger in October–November.

Pandit ji's Important Note

This horoscope is a general prediction based on the Moon sign and the planetary transits of 2027. Every person's kundli, dasha and antardasha are different, so show your kundli before a big decision. For Scorpio, 2027 is a year of luck and courage – strengthen Jupiter's grace further through religion and good deeds.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How will 2027 be for Scorpio?

2027 is a lucky year for Scorpio. Exalted Jupiter stays in the house of fortune from 25 January to 25 June, boosts your career from the 10th house from 26 June to 26 November, and Rahu in the 3rd house gives courage and income all year.

Is there Sade Sati or Dhaiya on Scorpio in 2027?

No. In 2027 Saturn stays in Pisces and Aries, which are the 5th and 6th houses from Scorpio. So Scorpio has neither Sade Sati nor Dhaiya this year.

When will Scorpio get a promotion in 2027?

Jupiter stays in the 10th house from 26 June to 26 November 2027 – the best time for promotion and a higher post. From 3 June to 20 October Saturn in the 6th house brings victory in competition.

When will Scorpio get married in 2027?

25 January to 25 June 2027 and the period after 26 November are good for marriage. Get the exact timing fixed through kundli matching and dasha.

When will money come for Scorpio in 2027?

5 July to 23 August 2027 (Mars in the house of gains) and the period after 26 November (Jupiter in the house of gains) are the best times for earnings.

What are the lucky number and colour for Scorpio in 2027?

Lucky numbers are 9 and 3, lucky colours are red, maroon and saffron, and the lucky day is Tuesday.
