Rahu-Ketu Transit 5 December 2026: Rahu Enters Capricorn, Ketu Enters Cancer – Effects on 12 Signs and Remedies

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: On 5 December 2026 at 10:32 PM, Rahu moves into Capricorn and Ketu into Cancer – a change lasting about a year and a half. Here is the effect on every sign from Aries to Pisces, with easy remedies. हिंदी में पढ़ें

Rahu and Ketu are called shadow planets – you cannot see them, but when they change signs, life takes turns nobody expected: a sudden job change, a chance to go abroad, an old relationship breaking or a big gain from a stranger. Now both are changing places.

When and Where Are Rahu and Ketu Moving?

By the traditional (mean node) calculation used in household panchangs, on Saturday, 5 December 2026 at 10:32 PM Rahu leaves Aquarius and enters Capricorn, and at the same moment Ketu leaves Leo and enters Cancer. Rahu and Ketu always move backwards and stay about a year and a half in one sign, so this change affects all of 2027 and the first part of 2028.

Note: some panchangs use the true node, which shows this change in the last week of November. We follow the mean node, like household panchangs, so 5 December.

What Rahu in Capricorn Means

Capricorn is Saturn's sign – hard work, discipline, jobs, government and big institutions. Rahu here makes ambitions big: a higher post, a bigger company, a name in politics. Those who work hard on the right path can rise fast; those who chase shortcuts, lies or bad deals can get a heavy jolt later.

What Ketu in Cancer Means

Cancer is the Moon's sign – mother, home, mind and emotions. Ketu here can bring detachment, restlessness and a pull towards spirituality, along with changes related to home and family.

General Remedies for Rahu-Ketu

  • Chant 'Om Raam Rahave Namah' and 'Om Kem Ketave Namah' 108 times.
  • Read the Durga Chalisa, especially on Saturdays.
  • Feed dogs and help those in need.
  • Never wear Gomed (hessonite) or Lehsuniya (cat's eye) without getting your horoscope checked.

These are general results by Moon sign. Your own chart, its houses and the running dasha decide the actual result.
