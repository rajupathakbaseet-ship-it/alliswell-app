Jupiter, Mars and Ketu Together in Leo (2026): Key Dates, Effects on All 12 Signs and Remedies

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 19 November 2026WfXT
In short: Jupiter, Mars and Ketu are together in Leo: Ketu leaves on 5 December, Jupiter turns retrograde on 13 December and returns to Cancer on 25 January 2027. A blessing for some signs, a test for others. हिंदी में पढ़ें

There is an interesting sight in the sky right now. In Leo, the sign of the king, Jupiter (the guru of the gods), Mars (the commander) and the mysterious Ketu are sitting together. When knowledge, strength and detachment meet in one place, astrology sees it as a time that changes people from within.

Planetary Positions (16 November 2026)

  • Jupiter: Leo, Magha nakshatra (about 1°41')
  • Mars: Leo, Magha nakshatra (about 1°52')
  • Ketu: Leo, Magha nakshatra (about 1°00', retrograde)

All three are within about one degree, in the same nakshatra, Magha. Magha is ruled by Ketu and is the nakshatra of the ancestors – so this period is linked to ancestral blessings, family property and old family matters.

What Changes Next – With Dates

  • 5 December 2026, 10:32 PM – Ketu leaves Leo for Cancer; the triple conjunction breaks and Jupiter-Mars remain in Leo.
  • 13 December 2026 – Jupiter turns retrograde in Leo.
  • 10 January 2027 – Mars also turns retrograde in Leo.
  • 25 January 2027 – Jupiter returns, retrograde, to Cancer (its sign of exaltation).
  • 13 April 2027 – Jupiter turns direct in Cancer.

Jupiter-Mars Conjunction: Guru-Mangal Yoga

Jupiter with Mars is considered very auspicious – Jupiter gives Mars the right direction. It brings bold but well-thought decisions, gains in land and property, and honour for people in police, army, sports and administration. In Leo it acts especially on government, politics and leadership.

Ketu's Company – Blessing or Blemish?

Ketu either suddenly boosts the planet it sits with or leaves its result incomplete. With Jupiter it gives spiritual understanding – a pull towards worship, pilgrimage and deep study. With Mars it can increase anger, injuries and haste. So until 5 December be careful with vehicles, fire, electricity and sharp objects.

Magha – the Throne of the Ancestors

Magha means "great" or "wealthy"; its symbol is a royal throne and its deities are the ancestors. Planets in Magha give results linked to family tradition, ancestral property and family honour – so old partitions, the ancestral house or grandparents' property may suddenly come up. If your horoscope mentions pitru dosha, this is a very good time for charity in your ancestors' names.

Career, Politics, Business and Health

Leo is the sign of power and administration. People in politics, government jobs, police, army, sports or management will get the chance to speak strongly. But with Ketu present, do not sign any big contract in haste until 5 December – read the papers twice and take trusted advice. Leo rules the heart, back and spine; Mars with Ketu can raise blood pressure, injuries, burns and fever. If you take blood pressure or heart medicine, keep up regular check-ups and never stop medicine without your doctor's advice.

What Jupiter Retrograde Means

From 13 December, Jupiter is retrograde: old unfinished work returns – stalled studies, an old relationship or stuck money may come up again. It is a time to finish old work rather than start new. When Jupiter returns to Cancer on 25 January 2027, good days are likely to begin for Cancer, Pisces, Scorpio and Capricorn.

What to Do – and What Not to Do

  • Offer water and donate chana dal at a banana tree on Thursdays.
  • Offer sindoor and chola to Lord Hanuman on Tuesdays.
  • Feed someone in need in your ancestors' names – especially fruitful because of Magha.
  • Do not take big decisions in anger; think again after 5 December.
  • Do not argue with your father, teacher or senior officers.
  • Do not wear ruby or coral without advice – the wrong gemstone can backfire.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this conjunction inauspicious?

No. Jupiter with Mars is auspicious. Ketu is with them only until 5 December, and it signals caution, not fear.

What should Leo natives do most?

Offer water to the Sun daily, read the Hanuman Chalisa and control anger. Get the position of Jupiter and Mars in your chart checked.

When does Jupiter go back to Cancer?

On 25 January 2027, while retrograde; it turns direct in Cancer on 13 April 2027.
