Pisces Horoscope 2027 (Meen Rashifal 2027): Sade Sati, Big Gains from Rahu, Children, Marriage, Career and Month-by-Month Predictions

✍️ By: Pandit Raju Pathak🗓️ Updated: 17 November 2026WfXT
In short: How will 2027 be for Pisces (Meen) Moon sign? Sade Sati's middle phase is running and the last phase begins on 3 June, but Rahu in the 11th house increases income all year, and from 25 January to 25 June exalted Jupiter in the 5th house blesses children, studies and love. Full predictions with remedies. हिंदी में पढ़ें

For people with the Moon in Pisces (Meen rashi), 2027 is a year of both hard work and big gains. At the start of the year Saturn is in your own sign – this is the middle phase of Sade Sati, which tests patience and responsibility. From 3 June 2027 Saturn moves into Aries and begins the last phase, and the mind's burden lightens. The biggest good news is that Rahu stays in your 11th house (house of gains) all year – a very strong combination for earnings, big contacts and the fulfilment of wishes. Also, from 25 January to 25 June Jupiter, the lord of your sign, becomes exalted in the 5th house and supports children, studies, love and luck. Pandit ji says – Saturn will make you work hard, but Jupiter and Rahu will surely give you the reward.

Pisces 2027 at a Glance

• Best period: 25 January to 25 June 2027 (exalted Jupiter in the 5th house) and from 26 November (Jupiter in the 7th house)
• Gains: Rahu in the house of gains all year – income, networking and sudden profit
• Period to be careful: 24 August to 7 October (Mars in the 8th house) and 26 June to 26 November (Jupiter in the 6th house)
• Children and love: Ketu in the 5th house – worry about children or distance in love; Jupiter will handle it
• Sade Sati: middle phase from January to 2 June; last phase from 3 June to 20 October; middle phase again from 20 October

Now let us see which house of Pisces each of these planets occupies and what results it gives.

Pisces Sade Sati 2027 – Which Phase and When

Pisces Sade Sati has been in its middle phase since 29 March 2025, because Saturn is in your own sign. According to the panchang, Saturn moves into Aries on 3 June 2027, your house of wealth, and the last phase of Sade Sati begins. Saturn turns retrograde on 9 August and returns to Pisces on 20 October 2027, so the middle phase runs again from 20 October to 22 February 2028. From 23 February 2028 the last phase becomes permanent and lasts until 2029.

In the middle phase, tiredness, delays in work and mental pressure are common. In the last phase, money, family and speech are tested. In both, punctuality, honesty and respect for elders keep Saturn pleased.

Career and Job 2027

With Saturn in your sign, responsibility at work will rise a lot, and at times you may feel you are not getting recognition in proportion to your effort. Be patient – what Saturn gives is lasting. Rahu in the house of gains brings contacts with influential people, jobs through networking and chances of a salary rise. From 18 November to 26 December Mars stays in the 10th house – the fastest period of the year for promotion and a new post.

From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter stays in the 6th house – effort and opposition at the office may increase, but you will succeed in competitive exams and in attempts to change jobs. From 27 December exalted Mars joins Rahu in the house of gains – chances of big earnings at the end of the year.

Business 2027

2027 is a year of profit for traders – Rahu in the house of gains brings new customers, big orders and income from online sales. Mars is in the 7th house from 5 July to 23 August – do not argue with partners. From 26 November Jupiter in the 7th house brings new and trustworthy partnerships. After 3 June, with Saturn in the house of wealth, be careful about selling goods on credit.

Money and Finances 2027

Financially, Rahu is your biggest ally – all year there are chances of earning through many routes and sudden gains. From 25 January to 25 June exalted Jupiter from the 5th house also aspects the house of gains – good returns from investments and happiness related to children are possible. From 3 June to 20 October Saturn in the house of wealth puts pressure on savings, so stop unnecessary spending then. Mars is in the 8th house from 24 August to 7 October – be ready for sudden expenses, medical costs or expenses related to in-laws.

Love Life 2027

With Ketu in the 5th house, there may at times be distance, coldness or misunderstanding in love. But from 25 January to 25 June exalted Jupiter is also in the 5th house – sincere and serious relationships grow stronger and family approval may come. From 10 March to 25 April Mars is also in the 5th house – passion rises, but do not say anything in anger that you will regret later.

Marriage 2027

For unmarried Pisces natives, 25 January to 25 June 2027 is a good time – exalted Jupiter from the 5th house aspects your sign and also creates chances of a love marriage. From 26 November 2027 Jupiter enters the 7th house and creates the strongest marriage yogas – the end of 2027 and the start of 2028 are very auspicious for marriage. Saturn from your sign aspects the 7th house, so marriage may be slightly delayed, but the relationship will be lasting. From 5 July to 23 August (Mars in the 7th house) married people should avoid arguments.

Family and Home 2027

Ketu in the 5th house asks you to pay attention to your children's studies, health and behaviour – give time to your children. From January to June exalted Jupiter may bring good news related to children, such as a child's birth, an admission or success. From 3 June Saturn in the house of wealth increases family responsibilities – keep your speech sweet. Rahu in the house of gains brings help from elder siblings and friends.

Health 2027

In the middle phase of Sade Sati, tiredness, joint pain, trouble with the feet and lack of sleep are possible. From 26 June to 26 November Jupiter in the 6th house asks for attention to the stomach, liver and weight. From 24 August to 7 October Mars stays in the 8th house – drive very carefully then and do not be careless about injuries or surgery-like situations. Walk daily, drink more water and meditate. For any problem, see a doctor immediately.

Studies and Exams 2027

For Pisces students, 25 January to 25 June 2027 is a very good time – exalted Jupiter in the 5th house gives memory, understanding and success in exams. This is the best time for board exams, admissions and scholarships. Ketu in the 5th house can distract you – make a daily study routine. From 26 June to 26 November there are chances of success in competitive exams.

Pisces 2027 Month by Month

January: Saturn in your sign – more hard work. Exalted Jupiter in the 5th house from 25 January – auspicious for children, studies and love.
February: Rahu in the house of gains – new ways of earning.
March: Mars in the 5th house from 10 March – patience in matters of love and children.
April: Jupiter direct from 13 April – stalled plans move ahead. Victory over opponents from 26 April.
May: a good month for studies, investment and marriage talks.
June: last phase of Sade Sati from 3 June – the mind becomes lighter. Jupiter in the 6th house from 26 June.
July: Mars in the 7th house from 5 July – do not argue with your spouse or partners.
August: Saturn retrograde from 9 August. Mars in the 8th house from 24 August – great care with vehicles and health.
September: control expenses and be patient at work.
October: Mars in the house of fortune from 8 October – travel and religious activities. Saturn back in your sign from 20 October.
November: Mars in the 10th house from 18 November – chances of promotion. Jupiter in the 7th house from 26 November – marriage yogas.
December: exalted Mars joins Rahu in the house of gains from 27 December – big earnings.

Pisces 2027: Lucky Numbers, Colours and Days

• Lucky numbers: 3 and 7
• Lucky colours: yellow, sea green and light pink
• Lucky days: Thursday and Monday
• Ruling planet: Jupiter (Guru)

Easy Remedies for Pisces 2027

1. For Sade Sati, light a mustard-oil lamp under a peepal tree every Saturday and chant "Om Sham Shanaishcharaya Namah".
2. Worship Lord Vishnu every Thursday and donate yellow things (chana dal, turmeric, bananas).
3. For Ketu, offer durva grass to Lord Ganesha and feed rotis to dogs.
4. Recite the Hanuman Chalisa daily – Saturn's burden becomes lighter.
5. Help children and needy students with their studies – both Jupiter and Ketu will be pleased.

Dos and Don'ts in 2027

✅ Do:
• Take decisions related to studies, children and love marriage between January and June.
• Turn Rahu's gains into savings and safe investments.
• Be punctual and honest – Sade Sati will become easier.
• Move marriage talks forward after 26 November.

❌ Don't:
• Drive fast or take risky jobs in August–October.
• Lend money or spend needlessly between June and October.
• Ignore what your children say.
• Get into shortcuts or wrong work on anyone's advice.

Pandit ji's Important Note

This horoscope is a general prediction based on the Moon sign and the planetary transits of 2027. Every person's kundli, dasha and antardasha are different, so show your kundli before a big decision. For Pisces, 2027 is a year of turning hard work into gains – do not fear Saturn; Jupiter and Rahu are with you.
