How to find your number (mulank)

Add the digits of your date of birth (only the day) until one digit is left. For example, the 14th: 1+4 = 5; the 29th: 2+9 = 11, then 1+1 = 2. That is your number. Today's number is found by adding the full date (day + month + year).
