Rahu's 18 months in the ascendant and the troubles of Kumbh Rashi

People of Kumbh Rashi, Rahu had been seated in your ascendant (lagna) for 18 months, and it had robbed you of your sleep at night. The very person you trusted betrayed you. There was a constant shortage of money, and any work of yours would stop just as it was about to get done. Whoever you placed hope in was not standing by you. This period had greatly increased mental restlessness. For these 18 months, while Rahu was seated in the ascendant, Kumbh Rashi people became very troubled. On top of that is the effect of Shani's (Saturn's) Sade Sati, meaning your problems had not been ending continuously since 2018. Yes.

Big good news may come now, but one caution

But now you may receive a very big piece of good news, and this good news may be that this time will be an important time for you. But be careful! Let me add one more thing here: the place where Rahu is going, the 12th house, is the house of expenses, and it can increase expenses somewhat. So I will tell you remedies for that. Please do follow these remedies.

The nature of Kumbh Rashi and a situation like King Harishchandra's

People of Kumbh Rashi: hardworking, sensitive and caring towards their family. But sometimes, what happens is that the planets and nakshatras come into such positions that everything gets separated from you. Apply this to yourself, look at it and understand it. Your situation at this time has become like the situation of King Harishchandra. He lost everything. His wife, his children, even his house and property, everything was sold. King Harishchandra endured great pain. That same pain has been with you for these 18 months.

The effect of Shani's Sade Sati will now reduce

But look, the story does not begin here. We also have to add Shani's Sade Sati to it. Now, from December, as they say, Shani reduces its effect six months before, and this is very good news for you, that Shani's effect will also reduce now. Six months before, Shani is seated here in the house of wealth (dhan bhav) of your kundli. So Shani's effect will also reduce.

Rahu in the 12th house: the effect of Dhanishta Nakshatra

And Rahu is moving into the 12th house of your kundli. Now we will also consider where Rahu's aspects (drishti) will fall from the 12th house, and we will tell you very important remedies. First, let us understand which nakshatra Rahu is coming into here. Look, Rahu is coming into Dhanishta Nakshatra here, and Dhanishta Nakshatra is the nakshatra of Mangal (Mars). So what is Rahu? It will also bring gains in land-related work. What is Rahu? It will get you a job again. Rahu will set right again the relationships that had turned sour.

The 12th house: relationships, foreign travel and work abroad

Because the 12th house is also said to represent your relationships. It is also linked to your foreign travel. If Rahu is in the 12th house of your kundli, take it that you will stay away from your birthplace. Wherever your birthplace may be, you will stay away from it. Otherwise, if Rahu is properly seated in the 12th house of the kundli, and it is seen according to Lal Kitab, then Rahu in the 12th house always points towards foreign lands. It makes you leave your own country and travel abroad. It gets you shifted there. So this is good news for you, that Rahu is going into your 12th house. So most young friends, or anyone who had been waiting a long time to live abroad or to work abroad, or if you work online, then too [unclear] is hidden here for you.

Rahu's aspects from the 12th house

Now, if we look at Rahu, which of your houses is Rahu touching from here, from the 12th house? So: 1, 2, 3, 4 and five.

Rahu's first aspect: the house of comfort (sukh bhav)

Look, Rahu directly falls on your house of comfort (sukh bhav). So what will Rahu do here when it casts its aspect, when it looks at this house with its aspect? This is the house of comfort. Big plans may be made here for you: of buying a big vehicle or a big house, something for your mother's comfort, some jewellery or gadget for your wife's comfort; you may buy these, because Rahu will make you earn that money here. [unclear] expenses it will make you incur from here itself, right? So your problems will end, but expenses will increase a little. What I mean is, you have to pay attention to these. Make sure expenses do not weigh heavily on your pocket.

Rahu's second aspect: the sixth house (debt and job)

Now if we look at Rahu's second aspect, where is Rahu looking? At your sixth house, which is the house of debt and also of job. So those who have lost their job, or those whose problems continue and who are not able to get a job: after 5 December, as soon as Rahu leaves the ascendant of your kundli and goes into the 12th house, it will bring you very good results related to your job. It can get you a promotion in your job. From your job, your salary may increase. You may have very good relations with your boss. If any loan is running on you here, or there is a loan problem, or you have taken a loan from someone, that money too can be seen getting repaid here.

Rahu's third aspect: the eighth house

Now Rahu's third aspect will go to the eighth house. What is the eighth house? It is the house of secrets, of tantra knowledge, of astrology. So if you are working on these, working on the knowledge of astrology, are connected with astrology, or know about someone or want to know, then Rahu will take you deeper here. Here, ancestral land: some ancestral land of yours that you do not even know about, its [unclear] and it may come to light, which can bring you a good amount of profit. For Rahu, signs of gain will be seen here especially in these matters. Rahu will bring you gains in these things. There may be a little discord with your in-laws' side, because it is also the house of in-laws. So it will be good if you do not create differences with your in-laws. Because in-laws are also seen in the eighth house, there may be quarrels with your in-laws.

Ketu's influence: keep your plans secret

Now if we look at Ketu here: 1, 2, 3, 4 and five, Ketu will touch here. Ketu will touch your 10th lord (dashmesh). So in the karmesh, you need to keep your plans secret here. The more you keep your plans secret, the more benefit you will get. From 5 December, Rahu will work on your plans, and here Ketu too, sitting in your Magha Nakshatra, will work on your plans.

Ketu's second and third aspects

With its second aspect, where will Ketu look? At the 12th house. So Ketu in the 12th house: definitely the ancestors (pitru) or some members of your family, take their blessings. You can get very good results. And with the third aspect, where will it look? Now from the seventh house it will look into the ninth house, into the house of wealth (dhan bhav), on Meen Rashi (Pisces), where Shani is seated. So in this too, it can bring your expenses, your travels, in works connected with your family, and can bring you gains here.

Signs that stalled work will get done

What I mean is that Rahu can get done here all those works of yours that had been stuck until now. So as soon as Shani's Sade Sati ends, these signs of gain are forming for you.

Remedies for Kumbh Rashi

What should you do at this time? Listen to these remedies. They are very important.

Remedy 1: Eat sitting on the floor in the kitchen

Look, when you eat, do not eat sitting on a charpai (cot) or in front of the TV. As far as possible, wherever your kitchen is, eat sitting in the kitchen. This will benefit you. Tie a knot of my words (remember them firmly). From the day you start eating sitting in your kitchen, sitting on the floor, Rahu will benefit you.

Remedy 2: Fennel in a red cloth on Wednesday

And the second thing: on Wednesday, you have to take a red cloth. Put saunf (fennel seeds) in it. After putting the fennel in it, you can keep it under your pillow and sleep. This will bring big changes in your expenses.

Remedy 3: Rahu mantra chanting and arghya to Surya Dev on Sunday

And on Sunday you have to chant Rahu's mantras and offer arghya (water) to Surya Dev (the Sun God). Do this much and see. You will definitely see this strength of Surya and Rahu being received by you, and you will see great happiness coming into your life.
